Talk track

SCOR gives us a common language for the supply chain. Snowflake can connect the evidence and run the analytics, models and applications that support decisions across it: what to promise, where to source, how to balance supply, which exceptions to address and how to recover value. We can enter through a particular business decision or through shared data needs. The detailed map distinguishes platform configuration, applications we build and actions we integrate with execution. Success is measured in supply-chain outcomes, not in how many boxes we colour in.

What this slide means

Orchestrate is Level 0. Plan, Order, Source, Transform, Fulfill and Return are Level 1. Plan spans the page to communicate its cross-process scope; it is not a higher hierarchy level. The aligned operational panels are not a mandatory sequence. Proposed applications use verified platform building blocks, but are not claims of turnkey SCOR functionality.

Sources and boundaries

  • ASCM, SCOR Digital Standard Quick Reference Guide, 2025, supplied PDF, pp. 2–5. Full SCOR standard. Original source carries CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; no source artwork reproduced. Review reuse permissions before commercial publication.
  • Snowflake platform overview: data, analytics, application and collaboration building blocks.
  • Snowflake forecasting: time-series forecasts, features, evaluation and intervals provide a component for demand planning. End-to-end planning workflows, causal promotion measurement and constraint-based supply planning require additional application logic, analytical methods and business data, which can form part of a solution built on Snowflake.
  • Snowflake AI and ML: assistance and custom model capabilities; outputs need appropriate evaluation and oversight.
  • Snowpark Container Services: runtime for custom applications and libraries; specialised optimisation models and solvers must be supplied and validated.

No ROI, customer implementation or account-specific deployment is asserted. SCOR outcome attributes are strategic groupings, not individual metrics. The companion mapping names candidate measures without claiming official process-to-metric linkages.
