The digital chain matters

Reshoring a factory does not remove dependence on an overseas planning service or AI provider. Alternative suppliers offer little protection if the information needed to switch production becomes unavailable.

Sovereign resilience means designing both chains together: map dependencies, govern location and access, and test recovery. Residency, jurisdiction and the ability to change course belong in the same strategy.

Regulation is converging

  • In Europe, NIS2 links supply-chain security with business continuity for in-scope organisations.7
  • GDPR sets conditions for personal-data transfers outside the EEA.8
  • The AI Act sets phased data-governance and human-oversight requirements for high-risk systems.9

Their scopes and timelines differ, but the operational response overlaps: map dependencies, govern information and evidence the controls. Supply chain, IT and legal need one strategy, not separate programmes.
