What is pharmaceutical supply chain?

The sourcing, production, release and distribution of regulated medicines. Products are made in batches under GxP, released by a Qualified Person, serialised for verification and often shipped in a temperature-controlled cold chain to wholesalers, hospitals and pharmacies. Every batch must be traceable end to end, and supply continuity for critical medicines leaves no room for substitution, which makes batch traceability, cold chain integrity, release speed and regulatory compliance the central challenges.

Persona 01 · Economic Buyer

Chief Supply Chain Officer / SVP Supply Chain

Their world

Owns the end-to-end supply chain from API suppliers and CMOs through sites, 3PLs and wholesalers to hospitals and pharmacies. One operating model has to satisfy several regimes at once: EU FMD in Germany, France and Spain, Bollino farmaceutico in Italy, MHRA rules in the UK and DSCSA in the US. A cold chain excursion, recall or serialisation failure is both a P&L event and a regulatory event, and supply continuity for critical medicines is non-negotiable.

What they care about

  • Seeing an excursion before the batch is lost, not after
  • Tracing any batch in hours, not days, when a recall is raised
  • Supply continuity for critical medicines, with shortages seen weeks ahead
  • Less near-expiry write-off and safety stock without risking patient supply
  • Turning AI investment into decisions that work within GxP

How they’re measured tap for definition

  • OTIF to Hospitals & Pharmacies

    On Time In Full: share of customer orders delivered on the committed date and in the full quantity.

    Orders on time and in full ÷ total ordersHeadline customer service KPI
  • Cold Chain Compliance

    Share of temperature-controlled shipments delivered without an excursion outside the labelled range.

    Shipments without excursion ÷ cold chain shipmentsEvery miss is a quality event
  • Recall Trace Time

    Time to identify every affected lot, its components and where each unit was shipped once a recall is raised.

    Hours from recall decision to complete traceSpans ERP, QMS, MES and serialisation
  • Days on Hand / DIO

    Days of inventory held, usually tracked by product category and market.

    Inventory value ÷ daily COGSBalanced against continuity of supply
  • Near-expiry Write-off

    Value of stock destroyed or written down because it reached, or came too close to, expiry before sale.

    Expiry write-off value ÷ total inventory valueShelf life drives the trade-off

Persona 02 · Business Champion

VP Technical Operations / Site Operations

Their world

Owns batch production across sites under GxP and cGMP. QMS, MES, LIMS and ERP each hold a fragment of the batch record, and none spans them all. A deviation means weeks of manual data gathering for root cause, and every batch that fails right-first-time costs capacity, cost and a compliance event.

What they care about

  • Higher batch right-first-time across every site
  • Faster deviation and root-cause investigation
  • Shorter batch release cycle time
  • Cross-site view of OEE without manual data stitching
  • Less rework, batch rejection and emergency changeover

How they’re measured tap for definition

  • Batch Right-First-Time

    Share of batches completed and released without a deviation, rework or reprocessing.

    Batches released without deviation ÷ batches startedFailures cost capacity and compliance
  • Batch Release Cycle Time

    Elapsed time from end of production to QP certification and release for sale.

    Days from batch end to releaseWaits on QMS, LIMS and record review
  • OEE

    Overall Equipment Effectiveness: how much of planned production time is truly productive.

    Availability × performance × qualityChangeovers and cleaning weigh heavily
  • Schedule Attainment

    Share of planned batches completed on the scheduled day and in the scheduled quantity.

    Batches to schedule ÷ batches scheduledMisses ripple into supply continuity
  • Deviation Rate

    Number of deviations raised relative to batch volume.

    Deviations per 1,000 batch recordsLeading indicator for release delays
