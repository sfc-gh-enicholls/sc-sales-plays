What is Pharmaceutical Supply Chain?

The manufacturing, quality management, and distribution of regulated drug products and medical devices. Pharmaceutical companies use batch manufacturing (under GxP/cGMP) and distribute through hospitals, pharmacies, and specialty channels, which makes regulatory compliance, batch traceability, cold chain integrity, and demand sensing the central supply chain challenges.
