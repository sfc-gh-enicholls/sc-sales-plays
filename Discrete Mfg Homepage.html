About this Play

Discrete manufacturing is the production of distinct, countable units assembled from a Bill of Materials — where each item can be tracked, reworked, or recalled individually. Sub-industries include automotive, industrial, aerospace & defence, medical devices, and electronics. These manufacturers run planning, execution, and quality across dozens of disconnected systems that were never designed to talk to each other. Every decision starts with reconciliation, not action. AI pilots fail because the underlying data is fragmented, ungoverned, and spread across siloed tools.

This play positions Snowflake as the intelligence layer that connects every system the customer already runs — so supply chain practitioners can build their own forecasts, risk models, and optimisation logic without waiting for IT to build pipelines. No rip and replace. No 18-month integration programme. Governed data, AI that works in production, decisions in seconds instead of days.

These materials give you the narrative, personas, and proof points. You still need to do your own discovery and tailor the story to each customer's specific pain, systems landscape, and transformation stage.
