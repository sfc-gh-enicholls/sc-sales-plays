ICP Quick Scan
Green Signals — Call These
$500M+ revenue (ideal: $1B+)
3+ retail customers (Walmart, Target, Amazon, Kroger, Tesco)
SAP or Oracle ERP in place
Has a demand planning tool (Blue Yonder, o9, Anaplan, Kinaxis)
Active digital / AI transformation initiative
Heavy promotional spend (15%+ of revenue in trade)
Sub-industry: Food & Beverage, Personal Care, Household Products, Confectionery, Beverages, Pet Care
ICP Quick Scan
Red Flags — Deprioritise
Revenue <$200M with single retail customer
No demand planning function — purely order-driven
Private label / own-brand only with no retailer complexity
No cloud infrastructure or IT modernisation plans
Pure B2B distribution with no consumer-facing products

New CDO, VP Data & Analytics, or Chief Digital Officer hired
SAP S/4HANA migration announced or underway
Earnings call mentions fill rate issues, retailer chargebacks, or demand volatility
New CSCO, VP Supply Chain, or VP Demand Planning appointed

New product launch announced — NPD is a demand forecasting stress test
▲ Entry point:

Target CSCO, CIO/CDO, or CPO as the primary sponsor. The “bring capability to the data” platform pitch resonates at C-suite level. VP Demand Planning, Head of S&OP, and Head of Supply Planning are valuable for qualification and discovery but typically don’t champion cross-functional change — use them to build the case upward, not as standalone deal owners.

The 30-Second Pitch
For CSCO / SVP Supply Chain

Most CPG companies plan trade promotions in one system and supply in another — and they never talk to each other. Your supply team gets surprised by demand spikes every promotional cycle. Snowflake unifies your ERP, demand planning, TPM, and retailer POS data into one governed platform so AI can actually work — and your team can see the full demand picture before it hits your DC. Kraft Heinz did this — they reduced out-of-stocks from 20% to 6% in 4 weeks.

The 30-Second Pitch
For CIO / CDO / VP Data

Most CPG AI pilots for demand sensing fail in production — not because the algorithm is wrong, but because retailer EPOS data is fragmented and ungoverned. Snowflake connects SAP, demand planning, TPM, and syndicated data into a single governed semantic layer. Demand accuracy, case fill rate, trade ROI — defined once, trusted everywhere. That's what makes AI actually reach production.

The 30-Second Pitch
For VP Demand Planning / Head of S&OP — champion-level contact

When trade promotion plans change, your supply team finds out when DC stock runs out — not 6 weeks earlier. Snowflake connects your TPM system and ERP so Cortex AI sees promotional lift and supply constraints simultaneously. One S&OP view everyone trusts — demand, supply, and finance aligned. Decision cycle drops from 3 days of pack prep to seconds of self-service. Use this to build the case to your CSCO.

Discovery Questions
CSCO / SVP Supply Chain
Opener: "When you last had a major promotion, how far in advance did supply know the full demand picture?"
"How many systems does your team reconcile before making a supply planning decision today?"
"How would you describe your team's confidence in the AI investments you've made in demand sensing so far?"
Discovery Questions
VP Demand Planning / Head of S&OP
Opener: "What percentage of your S&OP prep time is spent building the pack versus making decisions in the meeting?"
"When a major promotional plan changes, how quickly does that update reach your supply and procurement teams?"
"How accurate are your promotional lift forecasts today — and what does a 5-point MAPE improvement mean for your inventory position?"
Discovery Questions
CIO / CDO / VP Data
Opener: "Of the AI or ML pilots you've run in demand planning, how many have made it into production?"
"If your CSCO asks a supply chain question, how long before they get a trusted answer from your data team?"
"Do your demand, supply, and finance teams work from the same definition of 'demand plan' and 'case fill rate' — or do they argue about numbers in S&OP?"
Discovery Questions
CPO / Head of Supply Planning
Opener: "When a retailer chargeback hits, how long does it take your team to trace the root cause back to a demand signal or supplier miss?"
"What percentage of your safety stock is held to cover demand forecast uncertainty versus genuine supply risk?"
"How quickly can your team respond to a supply disruption with a re-plan that finance and commercial both trust?"

Objection Handling
Common Objections & Responses
"We're already committed to [Blue Yonder / o9 / Anaplan] for demand planning."
Snowflake is not a replacement for your planning tool — it's the data layer that makes it work better. Blue Yonder chose Snowflake as their preferred data foundation.
"IT won't support another platform to integrate and maintain."
Zero-copy means no new pipelines for IT to build or maintain. Your systems stay exactly where they are.
"We already have a data warehouse / data lake."
Snowflake likely works alongside it. The question is whether your demand planning, TPM, and retailer POS data actually trust what's in it.
"We're not ready for AI yet."
That's exactly why we start with the data foundation. Most aren't — the fragmentation between TPM and supply planning is the reason AI hasn't worked yet.
"Budget is tight — this isn't a priority right now."
Forrester found <6 month payback. Retailer chargebacks alone cost most CPG companies 1–2% of revenue. Supply chain is 40–60% of revenue — even a 1% improvement is tens of millions.
"We're evaluating alternatives that do everything in one platform."
Closed platforms lock your data in. Snowflake is open — your stack stays yours. Blue Yonder, o9, and Anaplan all integrate natively with Snowflake.
