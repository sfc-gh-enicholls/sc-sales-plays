What is Fashion Retail?

Brands and retailers that design, buy, and sell apparel, footwear, and accessories across wholesale, owned stores, and e-commerce. Success hinges on committing to inventory 6–9 months ahead of an uncertain trend, then chasing or marking it down in-season — making buying decisions, sell-through visibility, and markdown control the central commercial challenges.

PERSONA 01

Commercial Director / Chief Commercial Officer

Business Context

Owns the trading calendar: what gets bought, how deep, and when it gets marked down. Every season is a bet placed 6–9 months out, with no reliable read on how the range is landing until it's too late to act cheaply. Terminal stock and markdown depth are the two numbers that make or break the year, and both are discovered late.

What They Care About
Seeing sell-through by style, size, and store in near-real time, not next Monday's report
Reducing terminal stock and markdown depth at season end
Chasing winners into supply while there's still season left to sell them
Making AI investments pay off, when trend-sensing pilots keep failing
Protecting full-price sell-through as the primary lever on gross margin

PERSONA 02

Merchandising Director / Head of Merchandising

Business Context

Owns the OTB commitment, intake phasing, and the WSSI. Primary challenge: OTB is locked 6–9 months before the season, based on last year's numbers and a trend forecast, with no live signal to adjust against once the range is in market. The WSSI is rebuilt manually each week from exports across planning, e-commerce, and store systems.

What They Care About
A WSSI that reflects live sell-through, not a hand-built weekly export
One number everyone agrees on, ending buying vs merchandising vs finance reconciliation
Faster response to in-season trend shifts, so changes land in days not next month's reforecast
Accurate size-curve and store-level allocation for high-volatility styles
Less time building the WSSI and trading pack, more time making decisions

Business Context

Places the actual order: which styles, how deep, in which sizes and colours, from which vendors. Commits real money against a trend forecast made months in advance, then has limited room to chase a winner or cut a loser once the season starts. PERSONA 06 · ENTER AFTER THE TRADING DATA FOUNDATION IS ESTABLISHED

Sustainability Director / Head of ESG & Compliance

When to engage: Sustainability typically sits outside the trading decision chain, but CSRD, ESPR, and Digital Product Passport requirements are turning traceability into a board-level, audited obligation with real deadlines. The right time to open this conversation is after a Commercial Director or CIO/CTO sponsor has established the data foundation — product, supplier, and material data are then already unified and ready to extend into compliance reporting.

Business Context

Owns CSRD reporting, ESPR ecodesign compliance, and the coming Digital Product Passport mandate. Central frustration: traceability data (material origin, supplier certifications, carbon footprint) lives in fragmented supplier PDFs and spreadsheets, not systems. Deadlines are fixed by regulation, not by IT capacity.

What They Care About
Producing auditable CSRD disclosures without a six-month manual data-gathering exercise every year
Building the traceability foundation ESPR and DPP will require, before enforcement dates land
Connecting supplier and material data that currently exists only in PDFs and email
Giving buying teams supplier compliance data at the point of the buying decision, not after
Reducing terminal stock, which is itself a sustainability and waste metric increasingly scrutinised
