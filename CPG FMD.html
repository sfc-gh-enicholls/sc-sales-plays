OUR STARTING POINT

Supply Chain Stopped Being a Cost Centre. It Became the Strategy.

Four things now sit in your remit that used to sit somewhere else, or nowhere at all. Each one moved because the board needed someone accountable for it, and supply chain was the only function with line of sight across the whole business.

Growth
You decide what the business can credibly promise

Commercial can plan a promotion. Only supply can say whether it can be served, and what it costs to serve. That makes you a participant in the revenue plan, not a recipient of it.

Cash
You hold the largest lever on the balance sheet

Inventory is typically the biggest single line of working capital in a CPG business. When the CFO needs cash without touching price or headcount, the conversation comes to you.

Resilience
You are the function the board asks about risk

Tariffs, energy, freight, single-source ingredients, geopolitics. Continuity moved from an operational concern to a standing board item, and it landed with you.

Regulatory
You carry statutory obligations with dates attached

CSRD, EUDR and Digital Product Passport are law, not policy. They are traceability problems, which makes them supply chain problems, which makes them yours.

What changed

The mandate. Supply chain went from executing the plan to shaping it, and from reporting on the quarter to being asked what happens next.

What didn’t

The information base underneath it. You are being asked strategic questions on an operational data foundation, and closing that gap is the subject of the rest of this conversation.
