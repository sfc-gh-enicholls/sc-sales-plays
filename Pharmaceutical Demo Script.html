From Compliance to Confidence — Pharmaceutical scenario — Batch Genealogy · Cold Chain AI · Demand Intelligence

What we’re doing & how it relates to the play

“The regulation is the business case. You don’t have to build it.”

This demo is the conceptual centrepiece for the “From Compliance to Confidence” Pharmaceutical play. The play’s premise: across pharmaceutical supply chain, every major challenge — DSCSA/GxP compliance, cold chain reliability, batch quality, and demand sensing — is fundamentally a data fragmentation problem. Disconnected QMS, MES, LIMS, ERP, and cold chain IoT force teams to react to batch failures and compliance events after the fact; Snowflake unifies them into one GxP-governed platform so teams predict and prevent.

Positioning: From Compliance to Confidence helps pharmaceutical leaders unify batch records, cold chain, and demand data on a single GxP-governed platform — so every batch decision, compliance event, and demand forecast is made with confidence, not guesswork.

What this demo proves: one narrative event — a cold chain temperature anomaly (batch risk) colliding with a competitor drug shortage (demand upside) and an FDA suspect product notification (compliance urgency) — followed across four personas on Snowflake. It maps to the play arc: Compliance (batch genealogy + DSCSA — S1–2) → Intelligence (cold chain AI + demand sensing — S3–4) → Confidence (GxP-governed AI in production, FDA audit-ready).

Fictional Company — Demo Context
Company
Fictional large global pharmaceutical manufacturer
Multi-market branded pharma business with biologics and small molecule products across Global markets. Subject to DSCSA, EU FMD, and GxP/21 CFR Part 11 compliance obligations.
Supply Chain
Complex regulated manufacturing with QMS, MES, LIMS, ERP, and DSCSA serialisation systems. Biologics portfolio requires cryogenic cold chain across 3PL partners globally.
Planning Context
Batch manufacturing cycles of 14–60 days, DSCSA compliance obligations, and multi-channel demand from hospital, specialty pharmacy, and home health create persistent compliance and supply risk.

The Narrative Event — Three Simultaneous Pressures

Quality & compliance risk: A temperature excursion alert at a 3PL site may have compromised a biologics shipment. DSCSA requires suspect product response within 24 hours
Batch deviation flagged: A deviation was raised in QMS for a batch manufactured last week. Root cause trace requires records from QMS, MES, LIMS, and ERP
Demand upside: A competitor drug shortage has been announced, creating an opportunity to capture hospital formulary contracts and specialty pharmacy channel volume
