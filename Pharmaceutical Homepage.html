About This Play

Pharmaceutical companies across branded pharma, generics, biotech, and MedTech face compounding pressures: DSCSA and EU FMD traceability mandates, cold chain complexity, clinical and commercial demand uncertainty, and GxP-compliant data governance requirements. Every one is a data problem.

Snowflake solves this by connecting to every system the customer already has. Zero-copy, no rip and replace. One GxP-compatible governed platform for Compliance (batch genealogy, serialisation, audit trails), Intelligence (cold chain AI, demand sensing), and Confidence (unified QMS + MES + ERP + LIMS). Connect, extend, rationalise, on your timetable.

DSCSA (Drug Supply Chain Security Act) requires end-to-end serialisation and traceable chain of custody for every Rx drug unit from manufacturer to dispense. EU FMD mandates equivalent serialisation across Europe. Both make batch genealogy a legal obligation, not an IT project.
