CATEGORY 01

Fresh & Availability Performance

Fresh Waste %
Unsold Fresh Inventory as % of Fresh Sales
Industry range: 5–15% of fresh category value Target: <5%
The most direct measure of fresh category margin loss. Each 1% of fresh waste on £500M fresh sales costs £5M in pure margin — before disposal and markdown costs. Driven by demand forecast error, order inflexibility, and the inability to see the signals (weather, footfall, local events) that move fresh demand day-to-day.

Cortex AI unifies EPOS, cold chain, weather, and footfall data to predict fresh demand by store and shelf-life window. Replenishment and dynamic pricing recommendations before spoilage is inevitable.

Fresh Availability %
% of Time Fresh SKUs Are On-Shelf and In-Stock
Industry avg: 93–96% Target: 98%+
OOS in fresh categories is an immediate lost sale — a customer who can't find fresh chicken or salad at 6pm doesn't wait; they switch stores or to another format. Every availability failure in fresh is a loyalty event, not just a replenishment miss. Root cause: orders calibrated to averages that don't see demand signals.

Cortex Analyst: "Which fresh SKUs are at risk of OOS today across my South East stores?" — answered from live EPOS in seconds. Replenishment agent triggers before the shelf empties.

Demand Forecast Error (MAPE)
Mean Absolute % Error on Fresh Categories
Fresh perishables without AI: 25–35% MAPE Target: <12% MAPE
Fresh demand is highly non-linear — a 2°C temperature increase doubles salad sales; a local event can strip a bakery range in an afternoon. Statistical models calibrated on historical averages cannot see these signals. A 10-point MAPE reduction on £200M fresh sales eliminates millions in overstock, markdown, and disposal cost annually.

Cortex AI demand models retrain continuously on live EPOS, weather, footfall, and loyalty purchase signals from Snowflake Marketplace. Forecast accuracy compounds every season.

Replenishment Accuracy
Actual Units Ordered vs Demand-Optimal Order
Typical error: ±20% on perishable categories
The gap between what the replenishment system orders and what demand actually needed. In fresh grocery, overordering and underordering are equally damaging — one creates waste, the other creates OOS. Replenishment accuracy can only improve when demand signals feed directly into the ordering logic in near real-time.

Unified demand, weather, and EPOS data on Snowflake enables AI-driven replenishment recommendations per store per category — updated daily with live signals, not weekly averages.

Shelf-Life Pricing Uplift
Revenue Recovered Through Dynamic Shelf-Life Pricing
Target: recover 40%+ of potential waste value
When fresh items approach end of shelf life, dynamic markdown pricing can recover a significant fraction of margin that would otherwise be lost to disposal. Most grocery retailers apply blanket markdown rules too late — typically in the last 2 hours — because they lack real-time visibility into which SKUs at which stores are at risk.

Cortex AI monitors shelf-life window by SKU and store against demand forecast. Pricing recommendations surface 4–6 hours before the markdown window — maximising sell-through before disposal.

Cross-Channel Availability
Online vs. In-Store Inventory Accuracy
Omnichannel discrepancy: 8–15% of SKUs showing incorrect availability
When a customer orders online and their fresh items are substituted at pick, or they see OOS online while the store is fully stocked, it directly damages loyalty. The root cause is almost always that online OMS and store EPOS run as separate systems with no shared inventory layer.

Unified EPOS and OMS on Snowflake ensures a single inventory truth across all channels. Cortex Analyst: "What's the substitution rate for fresh produce picks in my online grocery operation?" answered from live data.

Spoilage & Disposal Cost

UK grocery: up to 15% of fresh category value written off Target: reduce 30–50% through AI forecasting
Spoilage cost includes not just the lost margin on unsold product, but also disposal cost, markdown labour, and sustainability impact. For a grocer with £500M in fresh sales, a 5% spoilage rate = £25M in annualised loss before disposal cost. Every 1-point improvement through better demand forecasting is pure margin. Source: WRAP UK / ECR Europe.

Cortex AI demand forecasting on unified EPOS, weather, footfall, and cold chain data reduces overordering at category and store level — the root cause of spoilage. Waste reduction in pilots: 8%+ per category.

Markdown Rate
% of Products Sold Below Full Margin
Fresh categories: 8–18% of volume sold on markdown Target: reduce unnecessary markdown 20–30%
Not all markdown is avoidable — but a significant proportion reflects demand forecast error rather than genuine excess. When replenishment over-orders because the signal was last week's average, the resulting clearance markdown is a pure data problem. Smarter forecasting and earlier shelf-life pricing reduce unnecessary markdown without sacrificing availability.

Unified demand forecast and cold chain data identifies markdown risk 4–6 hours before the window closes. Dynamic shelf-life pricing deploys at the right time — recovering margin that blanket end-of-day markdowns lose.

Shrinkage Rate

UK grocery average: 1.5–2.5% of revenue Target: <1.5%
Shrinkage — from theft, supplier short-delivery, and administrative errors — is one of the most margin-destructive problems in grocery. Most grocers lack the unified inventory view to separate waste, shrinkage, and unrecorded stock events at category and store level. Root cause analysis requires unified EPOS, WMS, and supplier delivery data.

Snowflake unifies EPOS, WMS, and supplier delivery records. Cortex Analyst: "Which stores and categories show anomalous shrinkage vs. the store cluster average?" — anomalies surfaced automatically.

Unified demand and supply data drives better inventory turns through more accurate positioning by category, store, and format. Cortex AI: "What is my days-of-supply risk for fresh produce across my convenience estate?" — answered from live data.
