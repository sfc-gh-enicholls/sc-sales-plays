The SC Pain — and Why Data Is the Root Cause
NPI — No History, Maximum Pressure

CPGs launch hundreds of SKUs annually. Idea to shelf in 3–6 months; cut after 3 if it underperforms. Zero demand history means the forecast is pure judgment. Over-produce = costly write-offs. Under-produce = lost distribution windows that retailers don't give back. Getting NPI right is the difference between a successful launch and a $20M inventory write-off.

Snowflake play

Cortex AI matches analogues (similar historical SKUs), combines retailer pre-order signals and marketing spend intent to build an NPI baseline before a single unit ships.

The SC Pain — and Why Data Is the Root Cause
Regulatory Compliance — Non-Optional

DSCSA (US) requires serialisation of every Rx drug unit and a traceable chain of custody from manufacturer to dispense. EU FMD / GDP mandates temperature audit trails. GxP / 21 CFR Part 11 requires data integrity and full audit logs with no exceptions. A product recall must trace every affected batch within hours — not days. The business case is pre-built by regulators.

Snowflake play

GxP-compatible governance layer. Batch genealogy query across QMS, MES, and ERP in a single unified query — recall trace reduced from 72 hours to 4 hours at named customers.

GxP-Compatible GovernanceBatch Genealogy
Cold Chain & IoT Complexity

Biologics and vaccines: 2–8°C. CAR-T cell therapies: −196°C cryogenic, patient-matched, with delivery windows measured in hours. A temperature excursion during 3PL transit = batch rejection + regulatory event notification + potential patient harm. Cold chain monitoring generates massive IoT sensor volumes across hundreds of 3PL sites that existing systems can't handle.
