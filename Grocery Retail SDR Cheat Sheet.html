The 30-Second Pitch
For COO / Operations Director

Most grocers order fresh food from weekly averages that can’t see tomorrow’s demand signals — weather, local events, footfall. So they oscillate between OOS in the afternoon and spoilage written off at the end of shelf life. Snowflake unifies EPOS, cold chain, weather, and loyalty data into one AI-ready platform so Cortex AI can predict fresh demand before it happens, not after you’ve ordered the wrong amount. 8% waste reduction. 97% availability maintained.

The 30-Second Pitch
For CMO / Chief Customer Officer

Your loyalty programme probably has tens of millions of members and extraordinary first-party data on what they buy, how often, and how price-sensitive they are by category. But that data is locked in a platform your buying team can’t access. Snowflake connects loyalty and EPOS into a single customer view — no separate CDP product. Personalised promotions built and deployed in hours. 3–5x ROI vs. generic offers. Loyalty data finally reaches the decisions it should be driving.

The 30-Second Pitch
For CCO / Head of Commercial

Your loyalty data is worth more than your current margin. CPG brands will pay premium rates to target and measure against your first-party audience — but only if you have a privacy-safe mechanism to share it. Snowflake Clean Rooms let brands run and measure campaigns without ever seeing your customer records. Albertsons built their retail media network on this. 70%+ gross margin on a new P&L line you already own the data for.

Objection Handling
Common Objections & Responses
“We already have a loyalty platform (Epsilon, Comarch, own-build).”
Snowflake is not replacing it — it’s the unified layer that connects loyalty to EPOS, buying, and retail media without moving the platform or the data.
“We have Oracle Retail / a data warehouse already.”
The question is whether loyalty, EPOS, cold chain, and e-commerce data actually connect in time to act on them. That’s where the value is.
“IT won’t support another platform to integrate and maintain.”
Zero-copy means no new pipelines for IT to build or maintain. Your EPOS, loyalty, and cold chain systems stay exactly where they are.
“We’re not ready for retail media yet.”
The data foundation is the prerequisite — starting with waste or loyalty gets you there. Albertsons started with unified data; retail media followed naturally.
“Budget is tight — margins are under pressure.”
That’s exactly the point. Every 1% of fresh waste reduction on £500M fresh sales = £5M. Retail media at 70%+ gross margin adds a new P&L line. Forrester found <6 month payback.
“We already share data with CPG brands through our portal.”
Portals give brands data but can’t prove campaign ROI or close the attribution loop. Clean Rooms do — which is why brands pay media rates, not trade rates.
