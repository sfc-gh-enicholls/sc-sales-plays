The Philosophy Difference

Retail SC: Starts from the customer — demand-driven, top-down. The product must find the customer. Inventory is the risk: too much = markdowns and write-offs; too little = lost sales and empty shelves. The WSSI, OTB and markdown frameworks are all tools for managing that stock risk in financial terms.   Manufacturing SC: Starts from capacity — supply-constrained, bottom-up. The order finds the product. Inventory is a buffer against uncertainty: safety stock, lead time, and MRP are the tools. These are opposite mental models. The vocabulary reflects it — learn retail's language before you walk in the door.
