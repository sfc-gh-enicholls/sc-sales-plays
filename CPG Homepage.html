About This Play

EMEA CPG leaders in food & beverage, personal care, household and pet care are being squeezed from every side: volatile demand, promotions planned without supply, retailer service penalties, tight working capital and AI that stalls in pilot. On top sit new traceability duties under CSRD, EUDR and the Digital Product Passport. Each one is a data problem: the answer sits across ERP, planning, TPM and retailer data that don’t talk to each other.

Snowflake connects to what the customer already runs, with no rip and replace: SAP data zero-copy via Business Data Cloud, everything else through connectors, Openflow and data sharing. One governed platform carries three jobs: Forecast (see true demand), Foresight (AI and scenarios that act before the disruption) and Proof (governed, auditable numbers for retailers, finance and regulators).

Acronyms. CSRD: EU sustainability reporting, including Scope 3 and supply-chain impacts. EUDR: EU deforestation rules for cocoa, coffee, palm oil, soy and more. DPP: product-level traceability data. Scope and dates are still moving under the EU Omnibus, so check the current position before quoting a deadline.
