About This Play

EMEA CPG companies — food & beverage, personal care, household products, confectionery, beverages — face compounding pressures: demand volatility, promotional chaos, AI stuck in pilot, retailer compliance, working capital constraints, and now mandatory CSRD sustainability reporting and Digital Product Passport traceability requirements. Every one is a data problem.

Snowflake solves this by connecting to every system the customer already has — zero-copy, no rip and replace. One governed platform for Forecast (demand planning), Foresight (AI & scenario modelling), and Proof (governance, compliance, ESG). Connect, extend, rationalise — on your timetable. Stop reacting to disruption and reporting deadlines. Predict them.
