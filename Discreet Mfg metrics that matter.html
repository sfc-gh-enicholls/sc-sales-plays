CATEGORY 01

Service & Delivery Performance

OTIF
On Time In Full Delivery
Industry avg: 75–85%Target: 95%+

The primary customer service KPI. Every percentage point gap represents lost revenue, customer penalty clauses, or both. For discrete manufacturers with contractual delivery commitments (automotive, aerospace), OTIF failure directly impacts revenue.

Snowflake unifies ERP, WMS, and supplier delivery feeds in real time. Cortex Analyst enables "what is OTIF for Plant NE this week?" in seconds — answered from live data, not last night’s batch.

Perfect Order Rate
Complete · On Time · Correct · Undamaged
Industry avg: 80–90%

Composite measure of order completeness, delivery timing, documentation accuracy, and product condition. A single failure in any dimension counts as an imperfect order. Drives customer satisfaction and repeat business in complex B2B discrete manufacturing supply chains.

Unified data layer correlates QMS quality records, ERP shipment data, and logistics events to automatically calculate perfect order rate across all dimensions — no manual cross-system reconciliation.

Order Fill Rate
Orders Shipped Complete from Available Stock
Target: 98%+ (MTS)

% of customer orders fulfilled completely from available inventory at first request. Critical for make-to-stock products. Partial fills increase logistics costs and damage customer relationships. Low fill rates are almost always a symptom of poor component availability visibility.

AI demand sensing on Snowflake improves component availability positioning, reducing stockouts that cause partial fills. Cortex AI early-warns on shortages 6–8 weeks before they impact fill rate.

Supplier OTIF
Supplier On Time In Full Component Delivery
Industry avg: 70–85%Target: 95%+

Your OTIF to customers is only as good as your suppliers’ OTIF to you. For discrete manufacturers with complex BOMs and sole-source components, a single supplier miss can stop a production line. Most companies track this weekly from EDI data — often 24–48 hours stale.

Snowflake’s zero-copy supplier data sharing brings live supplier delivery signals into the platform. Cortex AI scores supplier risk continuously — not just from EDI, but from 3,600+ Marketplace signals including carrier and port congestion data.

Customer Lead Time
Order Placement to Delivery
Varies by sub-industry

Time from customer order placement to delivery. A competitive differentiator especially in high-mix, configure-to-order environments. Lead time reduction drives revenue growth through improved win rates. Requires accurate, real-time visibility into component availability and production capacity.

Cortex Analyst provides instant visibility into available-to-promise (ATP) quantities across all sites — enabling faster, more confident lead time commitments without analyst intermediaries.

Forecast Bias
Systematic Over / Under Prediction
Target: ±5% at product family

Persistent forecast bias (consistently over or under) distorts inventory positioning and production scheduling across the entire supply chain. High bias is a compounding problem — each planning cycle amplifies the error. Root cause is almost always stale data inputs to the forecasting model.

Cortex AI retrains demand models on live order book, shipment, and market signals — automatically detecting and correcting systematic bias faster than any monthly planning cycle can.

Honeywell: tracks 3.5M SKUs against demand in near real-time — inventory matched to demand every hour across 55,000 suppliers, eliminating the data delays that cause OTIF failure  ·  ABB: eliminated month-long data reconciliation cycles across 40 ERPs

CATEGORY 02

Inventory & Working Capital

Inventory Turns
Cost of Goods Sold ÷ Average Inventory Value
Automotive: 8–15x · Industrial: 4–8x · A&D: 2–4x

Higher turns = less capital tied up in stock, less obsolescence risk, lower carrying costs. Industry benchmarks vary significantly by sub-vertical. Turns improvement of 1x on a $500M inventory base frees ~$60M in working capital. The lever is better demand/supply matching, which requires unified, real-time data.

ABB improved turns significantly after consolidating 40 ERPs — real-time supply/demand matching across 3.5M SKUs eliminated the excess buffer built to cover data latency.

Days Inventory Outstanding
Average Inventory ÷ (COGS ÷ 365)
Industry avg: 45–90 daysTarget: reduce 15–20%

Direct measure of how long capital is tied up in inventory. Every 10-day reduction in DIO on a $1B revenue manufacturer frees ~$27M in working capital. DIO is inflated by safety stock held to cover data uncertainty — when you trust your data, you need less buffer.

Cortex AI-driven demand sensing and live supplier visibility reduces the uncertainty that forces excess inventory holding. Governed semantic layer means finance, supply, and operations agree on the same inventory number — no padding for reconciliation uncertainty.

Safety Stock Level
Buffer Inventory Held Against Uncertainty
Target: right-sized, not minimised

Safety stock is rational insurance against demand and supply variability — the problem is when it’s sized to cover data uncertainty rather than genuine business risk. Most discrete manufacturers carry 15–30% more safety stock than necessary because they don’t trust their supply signals or demand forecasts.

Snowflake Marketplace live supplier signals + Cortex AI continuous risk scoring enables right-sizing safety stock by actual supplier risk — not as a blanket hedge against data lag. Hold less where risk is low, more where sole-source exposure is real.

E&O Inventory %
Excess & Obsolete Inventory as % of Total
Best-in-class: <2% · Industry avg: 5–10%

Excess and obsolete inventory represents sunk capital and future write-off risk. In discrete manufacturing, E&O is driven by demand forecast errors, engineering changes that obsolete components, and poor visibility into aging stock. An average $1B manufacturer writes off $10–50M annually in E&O.

Cortex AI detects aging inventory and near-obsolescence risk from ECO data in PLM before write-offs materialise. Unified BOM and inventory visibility across all sites enables inter-site transfers to consume at-risk stock.

Cash-to-Cash Cycle Time
DIO + DSO − DPO (days)
Industry avg: 45–90 daysTarget: reduce 10–20%

The CFO’s primary supply chain metric. Measures how efficiently the business converts cash into revenue and back. Every 10-day improvement on a $2B business frees ~$55M in cash. Driven by inventory reduction (DIO), faster collections (DSO), and better payment terms (DPO) — all enabled by supply chain data clarity.

Snowflake connects supply chain and financial data in one platform — enabling real-time cash-to-cash tracking and scenario modelling for working capital optimisation decisions.

Inventory Carrying Cost
25–35% of Annual Inventory Value
Carrying cost rate: 25–35%/yr

The true cost of holding inventory: capital cost + storage + handling + insurance + obsolescence risk. A manufacturer with $500M in average inventory pays $125–175M per year just to hold it. Even a 10% inventory reduction saves $12–17M annually in carrying costs — before counting the freed working capital.

Better demand/supply matching through unified Snowflake data and Cortex AI directly reduces average inventory held — and with it, carrying costs. ABB: $200M+ annual savings includes significant carrying cost reduction.

ABB: $200M+ annual inventory savings & $900M revenue growth influenced after consolidating 40 ERPs on Snowflake  ·  Forrester TEI: Average 25% reduction in inventory carrying costs across Snowflake supply chain deployments

CATEGORY 03

Production & Manufacturing Excellence

OEE
Overall Equipment Effectiveness
Industry avg: 65% · World-class: 85%+

The composite production efficiency metric: Availability × Performance × Quality. A 1% OEE improvement on a 100-unit/hr line running 8,000 hrs/year adds 8,000 units of capacity without capital expenditure. Most discrete manufacturers leave 15–20% OEE on the table due to undetected degradation and poor quality data.

Cortex AI detects OEE degradation patterns in SCADA and MES data before they cause downtime. Schneider Electric: real-time OEE insights across 290+ smart factories — enabling proactive maintenance that keeps OEE at world-class levels.

Schedule Attainment
Actual Production vs Plan (%)
Industry avg: 80–85% · Target: 95%+

The direct measure of whether manufacturing is executing to the plan. Low schedule attainment drives premium freight, customer penalties, and expedite costs. Root cause is almost always upstream — stale component availability data causing a plan that couldn’t be executed as written.

Live component availability from Snowflake feeds into production planning — schedules built on real data. Cortex AI flags component shortages before the schedule is locked, not after the line stops. Planning cycle time reduced from days to shifts.

First Pass Yield
Units Passing Quality Inspection First Time
Best-in-class: 99%+ · Automotive target: 99.5%+

% of units completing the manufacturing process without any rework or scrap. Every unit that fails first pass costs 2–4× the original manufacturing cost to remediate. FPY degradation is an early signal of process drift, component quality issues, or operator error — and it compounds quickly if not caught early.

Cortex AI connects QMS quality records with MES production data and supplier lot information to detect the root cause of FPY degradation in minutes — before it becomes a quality escape or recall event.

Scrap Rate
Defective Units as % of Total Production
Target: <2% · Automotive: <0.5%

Direct cost of quality failures. Scrap costs include material, labour, overhead, and lost capacity — often 3–5% of revenue in high-mix discrete manufacturing. Scrap rate spikes are usually linked to specific component batches, process parameter drift, or tooling degradation — all detectable earlier with connected data.

Unified QMS, MES, and supplier lot data on Snowflake enables automatic correlation of scrap events with incoming component batches, tooling data, and process parameters. AI anomaly detection triggers before scrap accumulates.

Unplanned Downtime
% of Scheduled Production Time Lost
Target: <2% of scheduled time

The most expensive manufacturing event. A single hour of unplanned downtime on a high-volume line can cost $10,000–$100,000+ depending on the product. Unplanned downtime from component shortages is particularly costly because it was preventable — the signal existed, just not in a form anyone could act on in time.

Cortex AI monitors live SCADA and MES streams for anomaly patterns predictive of equipment failure. Component shortage signals from unified supply data prevent line stoppages weeks before they would have materialised.

Capacity Utilisation
Actual Output vs Maximum Rated Capacity
Target: 80–85% (sustainable)

Running too low wastes capital investment. Running too high creates quality risk, burnout, and no room for demand surges. The sweet spot is 80–85% sustainable utilisation, which requires accurate demand forecasting, constrained scheduling, and real-time capacity visibility across all production lines simultaneously.

Snowflake unifies MES capacity data, production schedules, and demand plans in one query. Cortex AI enables scenario modelling for capacity allocation decisions across all sites — supporting load balancing without manual spreadsheet gymnastics.

Schneider Electric: real-time OEE and quality insights across 290+ global smart factories — Gartner #1 Supply Chain 4 consecutive years  ·  Honeywell: live production inventory tracked every hour across all global sites
