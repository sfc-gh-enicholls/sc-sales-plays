Help EMEA fashion retail leaders connect every consumer signal to every shelf decision — in-store, online, or anywhere in between — on one trusted data platform, so what you design, make, stock and fulfil is driven by real demand, not guesswork.

About This Play

EMEA fashion retailers — luxury, sportswear, high street, department stores, fast fashion, footwear — face six compounding forces: trend windows closing before weekly reports arrive, omnichannel inventory blind spots, AI personalisation stuck at pilot, a 30–40% markdown rate driven by disconnected buying data, nearshoring decisions made without cost or carbon data, and now mandatory CSRD reporting, ESPR circularity obligations, and Digital Product Passport traceability requirements. Every one is a data problem.

Snowflake solves this by becoming the intelligence layer on top of every system the customer already has — zero-copy, no rip and replace. One governed platform for Signal (consumer & demand intelligence), Shelf (assortment, fulfilment, personalisation), and Proof (DPP, CSRD, traceability). Turn demand signals into sell-through — and proof.
