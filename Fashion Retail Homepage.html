Signal to Shelf

Trends now move faster than the buy. Help EMEA fashion retail leaders connect demand signals, merchandise plans and inventory on one governed platform, so buying, allocation and markdown decisions follow real demand and full-price sell-through is protected before the season turns.

About this Play

Fashion retail covers luxury, sportswear, high street, department stores, fast fashion, footwear and outdoor. These retailers plan the season in one place (MFP, WSSI, open-to-buy), sell it in another (POS, ecommerce, loyalty) and source it in a third (PLM, supplier portals, Blue Yonder or SAP). Every system has its own number. Trend signals arrive in weekly reports after the window has closed, stock sits in the wrong stores and channels, markdowns land late and deep, and DPP and CSRD reporting depend on supplier data nobody has joined up.

This play positions Snowflake as the intelligence layer that connects every system the customer already runs, through connectors, Openflow and data sharing with suppliers and logistics partners (and SAP Business Data Cloud where SAP is the core). One governed platform for Signal (demand), Shelf (buying, allocation and markdown) and Proof (traceability and compliance). No rip and replace, and AI that reaches production instead of stalling in pilots.

These materials give you the narrative, personas, and proof points. You still need to do your own discovery and tailor the story to each customer's specific pain, systems landscape, and transformation stage.

The Play in 90 Seconds · Signal to Shelf

Flare jeans are selling out in three cities while skinny stalls. The planning team sees the signal, changes the plan once, marks down early and shallow, and signs off commercial and compliance together. A person makes the call. Illustrative scenario.

Play at a Glance
Target
EMEA fashion retailers with £500M+ revenue, multi-channel operations, seasonal buying complexity and DPP / CSRD obligations
Sub-verticals
Luxury · Sportswear · High Street · Department Store · Fast Fashion · Footwear · Outdoor & Lifestyle (examples, not exhaustive)
Buyers
Economic: Commercial Director / CCO (CIO/CTO on larger platform deals)  ·  Champions: Merchandising Director, Buying Director, Supply Chain & Sourcing, Allocation & Replenishment  ·  Influencers: CFO, Ecommerce Director, Sustainability
Core pain
Plan, sell and source data in separate systems. Trends spotted after the window closes. Stock in the wrong place, markdowns late and deep. DPP / CSRD traceability that depends on supplier data nobody has joined up.
Our angle
Signal · Shelf · Proof on one governed platform. Intelligence layer on top of the systems they already run. Merchandisers and planners make better calls on the WSSI. No rip and replace.
Readiness
Multi-channel, MFP or WSSI in place, a funded data/AI or replatforming initiative. Single-channel or spreadsheet-only planning → likely too early.
