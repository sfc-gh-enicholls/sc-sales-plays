From Forecast to Foresight — CPG scenario — Consumer Packaged Goods

What we’re doing & how it relates to the play

“Stop reacting to disruption and reporting deadlines. Predict them.”

This demo is the conceptual centrepiece for the “From Forecast to Foresight” CPG play. The play’s premise: across EMEA CPG, every major supply-chain theme — cost & margin pressure, resilience & agility, AI demand forecasting, and traceability — is fundamentally a data problem. Fragmented systems force leaders to react to disruption after the fact; Snowflake collapses that into one governed platform so leaders predict and respond.

Positioning: From Forecast to Foresight helps EMEA CPG leaders turn fragmented supply-chain data into a single, trusted intelligence layer — so every decision, from demand to compliance, is made with foresight, not hindsight.

What this demo proves: one narrative event — a competitor recall (demand upside) colliding with a drought at a key ingredient supplier and a delayed bottling-line commissioning (supply risk) — followed across four personas, all on Snowflake. It maps to the play’s arc: Forecast (AI keeps the base plan current + quantifies new demand — S1–2) → Foresight (AI surfaces risk early, dollar-quantifies it, runs optimised scenarios — S1, 3–4) → Proof (bridge: same platform underpins traceability + CSRD/DPP — not demonstrated below; add a closing slide).

Fictional Company — Demo Context
Company
Fictional large EMEA CPG manufacturer
Multi-category branded goods business operating across the EMEA region.
Supply Chain
Complex multi-tier supply chain with ingredient sourcing exposure, manufacturing constraints, and retailer service-level commitments.
Planning Context
Seasonal demand patterns, promotional planning complexity, and ingredient supply exposure create persistent forecast and fulfilment risk.

The Narrative Event — Multiple Pressures Converging

Demand upside: A competitor product has been recalled, creating an opportunity to capture shelf space and a major retailer contract
Supply risk 1: New bottling line commissioning delayed due to resource and engineering constraints
Supply risk 2: Extreme weather (drought) affecting a key agricultural ingredient supplier, creating shortages in coming weeks/months

Section 1 — SC Health & Alerts

Persona: Head of Supply Chain (Planning)
Scene 1a · SC Health Overview
Setup
The exec starts their day in a single control tower dashboard — no more pulling reports from disparate systems.
What to show
  • Control tower with at-a-glance KPIs: service level, inventory, forecast accuracy
  • Real-time AI insights surfacing the areas that need attention most
  • Customizable alert thresholds (only what matters shows up)
Aha moment
Call out: AI-driven dashboard eliminates manual data aggregation. The exec isn’t reacting to reports — they’re managing by exception.
So what
Outcome: Proactive vs. reactive supply chain management. Hours saved on reporting.
Scene 1b · Key Alerts
Setup
System has flagged two critical developments this morning:
What to show
  • AI-powered alert feed, personalized and prioritized
  • System auto-quantifies risk in financial terms: “XX% revenue uplift in Q2 and Q3 at risk”
  • Instant visibility into financial consequences of inaction
Aha moment
Call out: System doesn’t just flag risks — it puts dollar values on them so leadership can prioritize fast.
So what
Outcome: Leadership is always informed of potential risks and understands severity before it becomes a crisis.
Scene 1c · Scenario Comparison
Setup
Given the risks and opportunities, the exec wants to evaluate options quickly.
What to show
  • Copy from base scenario, modify parameters, add comments — collaborative workflow creation
  • Automated workflow assigns tasks to demand, supply, and finance teams
  • Side-by-side scenario comparison on key metrics: revenue, margin, service level impact
Aha moment
Call out: Workflow creation is automated and linked to scenario creation — no manual task assignment, no delays. Comments route to the right owner immediately.
So what
Outcome: Faster decisions, better decisions — full business impact visibility, cross-functional alignment built into the workflow.

Section 2 — Demand Planning

Persona: Demand Planner
Scene 2a · Base Demand Forecasting
Setup
The demand planner needs to keep the base forecast current without drowning in manual adjustments.
What to show
  • Base demand forecasting dashboard with “auto-selected” best fit model highlighted
  • FCA (Forecast Contribution Add) value-add metric — highlights where planner input improves accuracy vs. hurts it
  • Focuses planner time on value-add areas only
Aha moment
Call out: AI continuously updates the forecasting model. Planner only intervenes where they add value, as measured by FCA. It prevents interventions that reduce accuracy.
So what
Outcome: Higher forecast accuracy with reduced effort. Planner time freed for scenario modeling, action design, decision prep.
Scene 2b · Demand Opportunity Review
Setup
The competitor recall creates a new demand scenario to model. Planner needs to quantify volume and financial impact of capturing the retailer contract quickly.
What to show
  • Scenario planning dashboard with real-time business impact visualization
  • AI agent surfaces relevant historical scenarios with related assumptions for the planner to build from
  • Planner tweaks opportunity parameters (margin, timing, volume, probability)
  • Comparison chart: volume/revenue impact vs. current targets
  • Immediate cross-functional task assignment to responsible demand planners
Aha moment
Call out: The AI agent enhances scenario modelling with insights from past cases. Immediate volume/financial visibility into potential revenue shifts.
So what
Outcome: Early identification of demand shifts enables ROI-backed decisions. Enables cross-functional alignment with supply and finance teams before plans are locked.

Section 3 — Supply — Gap Review & Bottleneck

Persona: End-to-End Supply Planner
Scene 3a · Supply Gap Review
Setup
The system has already identified new supply risks (feeding from the alerts in Section 1). Two specific bottlenecks are surfaced:
What to show
  • Solver re-runs automatically across all internal and external constraints for two scenarios (pessimistic and optimistic)
  • Supply gap dashboard showing projected gaps over time with respective bottlenecks
  • Initiative tracking: mitigation actions, their volume/financial impact, and ROI for management decisions
  • AI-powered recommendations and drill-down ranking for risk mitigation
Aha moment
Call out: AI-driven optimisation reduces manual effort. Early risk identification provides longer lead time and a broader solution set. System prioritises and recommends drill-down actions to minimise gap and maximise profit.
So what
Outcome: Minimises lost sales and excess inventory. Accelerates decision-making and enables timely responses to disruptions.
Scene 3b · Internal Bottleneck Drill-Down
Setup
The new bottling line commissioning has been delayed due to resource and engineering constraints. Planner drills into this from the supply gap overview.
What to show
  • Internal capacity drill-down dashboard: bottling line status, project timelines, production schedules
  • Planner identifies that reallocation of production line operators and shift capacity resolves some of the gap (requires management sign-off with trade-off evaluation)
  • ROI/cost/investment impact evaluations for trade-offs: labour costs vs. production gains vs. project prioritisation
  • Overtime adjustments on existing lines bridge remaining gap at higher cost
  • Both actions auto-documented as drafts, triggering action owners to review and financially evaluate
Aha moment
Call out: Internal bottleneck visibility is automated — real-time monitoring. Decision trade-offs are quantified for management.
So what
Outcome: Maximises production efficiency by reallocating constrained resources. Aligns operational decisions with strategic and financial trade-offs. Ensures company-wide optimisation.

Section 4 — Supply — External Ingredient Sourcing

Persona: End-to-End Supply Planner
Scene 4a · External Ingredient Sourcing Drill-Down
Setup
Drought conditions have disrupted the supply of a critical raw ingredient (e.g. hops, barley, or CO₂). Cascading effects are expected on the primary ingredient supplier in coming weeks/months. Planner analyses volume at risk and defines mitigation actions:
What to show
  • Sourcing drill-down dashboard: primary supplier constraints, at-risk order volumes
  • Upstream volume requirement analysis of the critical ingredient, visualised via ingredient supply chain network chart (from raw agricultural source through to finished product)
  • Financial trade-off comparison: cost of securing additional supply vs. supply assurance benefits
Aha moment
Call out: Proactive risk detection enables early mitigation. Scenario modelling quantifies the financial impact of securing additional inventory and stockpiling critical ingredients.
So what
Outcome: Enhances supply chain resilience through improved visibility and proactive risk management. Ensures demand fulfilment by securing supply ahead of potential disruptions.
Scene 4b · Supply Gap Review (Updated)
Setup
Planner reviews all newly created mitigation initiatives, categorised by scenario. Each scenario outlines specific actions from internal and external bottleneck analyses.
What to show
  • Supply gap dashboard with prioritised mitigation initiatives per scenario
  • Side-by-side scenario comparison: financial and operational impact
  • Approval workflow module for streamlined management decision-making
  • All risk-mitigation actions aggregated into structured, actionable initiatives
Aha moment
Call out: Seamless transition to executive decision-making. All actions aggregated and ready for the next dashboard (executive layer).
So what
Outcome: Provides a structured approach for resolving supply gaps, ensuring cross-team alignment. Enables data-driven management decisions, optimising resource allocation.
