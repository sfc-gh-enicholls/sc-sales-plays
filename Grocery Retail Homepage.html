Help grocery supply chain leaders turn fragmented EPOS, cold chain, and supplier data into a single AI-ready foundation — cutting fresh waste, improving demand forecasting, and building the adaptive supply chain that protects margin.

About This Play

Grocery operates on a 2–5% trading margin, and the biggest single drag on that margin is supply chain. Fresh waste driven by demand forecast errors, replenishment calibrated to last week’s averages, and OOS events that play out in real time — none of these can be fixed without unified, AI-ready data across EPOS, cold chain, and suppliers.

Snowflake connects to the systems the customer already runs — SAP zero-copy through SAP Business Data Cloud, everything else through connectors, Openflow and data sharing — so replenishment gets better signals without being replaced. The primary play is supply chain: store-day-SKU demand forecasting, fresh waste reduction and supplier collaboration. Loyalty and retail media are expansion use cases on the same foundation, once it exists.
