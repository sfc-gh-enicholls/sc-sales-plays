The CSCO role has moved from running operations to shaping strategy. Three pressures drive that.

Geopolitical risk: McKinsey finds only 42% can see supplier risk beyond tier one, and month-long disruptions every few years can cost almost half a year's profits over a decade.

Slow alignment: 96% say getting cross-functional agreement takes hours or longer, because every decision starts with reconciling data.

AI failure: MIT's study found 95% of organisations getting no return from generative AI. Supply chain AI risks the same fate unless the data foundation is fixed.

Three ways to start; pick one, not all three. Start where the pain is greatest.

Demand intelligence: when demand signals arrive late. Decision: when to re-plan. Owner: head of demand planning.

Supply optimisation and risk: when supply shocks surface too late. Decision: where to re-source. Owner: head of procurement or supply.

Visibility and control tower: when delivery exceptions go unseen. Decision: which exceptions to handle first. Owner: head of logistics.

For each, agree today's baseline and a target. That becomes the workshop agenda.
