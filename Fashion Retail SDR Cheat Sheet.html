SDR Cheat Sheet: Fashion Retail
Signal to Shelf Play · Quick Reference for Prospecting & Discovery Calls
Internal Use Only
ICP Quick Scan
Green Signals · Call These
£300M+ revenue (ideal: £1B+)
Owned stores + e-commerce + wholesale, i.e. multi-channel trading complexity
SAP, Oracle Retail, or a legacy merchandising ERP in place
Has a planning tool (Blue Yonder, JDA, o9, Anaplan) but it’s not connected to POS/e-commerce data
Active digital / AI transformation initiative
Seasonal, trend-driven product range (2+ seasons/year, or fast-fashion cadence)
EMEA footprint subject to CSRD, ESPR, or upcoming Digital Product Passport requirements
Sub-industry: Athletic/Sportswear, Luxury, Fast Fashion, Department/High Street, Pure-Play E-com
ICP Quick Scan
Red Flags · Deprioritise
Revenue <£100M with a single, static, non-seasonal product line
No buying/planning function, purely reorder-driven basics business
Franchise-only model with no direct inventory or trading ownership
No cloud infrastructure or IT modernisation plans
Pure wholesale/B2B distribution with no consumer-facing retail or e-commerce
ICP Quick Scan
Trigger Events · Reach Out Now
New CIO, CTO, or Data Director hired
New e-commerce platform migration announced or underway
Earnings call mentions markdown pressure, terminal stock, or margin compression
New Commercial Director or Merchandising Director appointed
CSRD reporting deadline approaching, or ESPR/DPP programme kicking off
AI / trend-sensing programme publicly announced
Store estate restructuring or channel-mix shift announced, forcing a re-look at inventory allocation
New seasonal collection launch under a compressed buying window, a trend-sensing stress test
▲ Entry point:

Target Commercial Director, CIO/CTO, or Merchandising Director as the primary sponsor. The “bring capability to the data” platform pitch resonates at C-suite level. Use them to build the case upward, not as standalone deal owners.

Discovery Questions
Commercial Director
Opener: "You want to markdown early and shallow instead of late and deep. What’s stopping you today: the data, the tooling, or the timing?"
"How many systems does your team reconcile before making a markdown or chase decision today?"
"How would you describe your confidence in the AI/trend-sensing investments you've made so far?"
Discovery Questions
Merchandising Director
Opener: "How long does it take your team to build the WSSI each week, and how much of that is manual export and reconciliation?"
"When sell-through shifts mid-season, how quickly does that update reach buying and the trading meeting?"
"How accurate is your in-season reforecast today, and what does closing that gap mean for terminal stock?"
Discovery Questions
CIO / CTO / VP Data
Opener: "You want a semantic layer for AI to work on. Where does your trading data live today, and is it connected?"
"If your Commercial Director asks a sell-through question, how long before they get a trusted answer from your data team?"
"Do your buying, planning, and finance teams work from the same definition of 'sell-through' and 'markdown depth', or do they argue about numbers in the trading meeting?"
Discovery Questions
Buying Director
Opener: "When a style is outperforming plan, how long does it take to place a chase order with a vendor who can actually deliver?"
"How many of your vendors can you see real-time performance data for today: OTIF, quality, compliance?"

Discovery Questions
Ecommerce Director
Opener: "Your site search and browse data probably shows a trend shift before store POS does. How fast does that signal reach buying today?"
"If you migrated e-commerce platforms tomorrow, would you lose years of customer behavioural history, or does it live independently?"

Objection Handling
Common Objections & Responses
"We're already committed to [Blue Yonder / JDA / o9 / Anaplan] for planning."
Snowflake connects to your planning tool, not replaces it. Your planner keeps running your plan. Snowflake connects it to POS, e-commerce, and PLM data the planning tool can’t reach on its own.
"IT won't support another platform to integrate and maintain."
Zero-copy means no new pipelines for IT to build or maintain. Your systems stay exactly where they are.
"We already have a data warehouse / data lake."
Snowflake likely works alongside it. The question is whether your buying, planning, and e-commerce data actually trust what's in it.
"We're not ready for AI yet."
That's exactly why we start with the data foundation. Most aren't. The fragmentation between planning and store/e-commerce systems is the reason trend-sensing AI hasn't worked yet.
"Budget is tight, this isn't a priority right now."
Forrester found <6 month payback. Terminal stock and markdown depth alone erode gross margin every season. Even a few points of full-price sell-through recovered is tens of millions.
"We're evaluating alternatives that do everything in one platform."
A closed platform reaches a ceiling. It can’t ingest your POS, e-commerce, or partner signals. Snowflake connects, extends, then lets you rationalise what you no longer need, on your timetable.
