Quality & Compliance Metrics

Batch Rejection Rate
% of Batches Rejected Before or After Release
Industry avg: 2–5% of batches rejected Target: <1% for established products
The primary quality and cost KPI for pharmaceutical manufacturing. Every rejected batch is a direct loss: manufacturing cost, API destroyed, supply gap, and a potential regulatory event. Root cause investigation is typically manual, taking days to weeks across QMS, MES, and LIMS. For a company manufacturing $500M of product, a 1-point rejection rate improvement is $5M+ in direct savings.

Snowflake unifies QMS deviations, MES execution data, and LIMS results in real time. Cortex AI detects rejection risk patterns before batch release, enabling intervention rather than investigation.

OTIF to Hospital/Pharmacy
On Time In Full to Regulated Channels
Hospital/pharmacy OTIF target: 99%+ Patient supply: zero tolerance for stockout
OTIF failures in pharmaceutical carry consequences beyond revenue loss: drug shortages are reported to the FDA, and repeated failures can trigger enforcement action. For critical medicines with no substitute, a supply failure has direct patient impact. Root cause is nearly always demand uncertainty combined with batch scheduling disconnected from channel demand signals.

IQVIA prescribing data, hospital purchasing signals, and payer formulary changes from Snowflake Marketplace connected to internal supply plan. OTIF risk detected weeks in advance, not when the order is already late.

Batch Right-First-Time %
% Batches Released Without Rework or Investigation
Industry avg: 85–92% right-first-time Target: 97%+ for established products
Right-first-time is the manufacturing quality metric that most directly drives batch release cycle time and COGS. Each batch requiring investigation or rework adds 3–21 days of hold time, delaying revenue and consuming quality resource. Cross-system root cause analysis is the bottleneck.

Cortex AI connects batch process parameters, in-process QMS checks, LIMS results, and historical deviation patterns. Predictive quality scoring flags at-risk batches before release hold, enabling proactive intervention.

Batch Recall Trace Time
Hours to Complete Chain-of-Custody Trace
Industry avg: 72+ hours manually Target: <4 hours (FDA expectation)
When an FDA recall or suspect product notice is issued, the manufacturer must provide a complete chain of custody for all affected lots within hours, not days. For most pharma companies, this requires manually extracting records from QMS, MES, ERP, and serialisation systems. Every hour of delay increases regulatory exposure and patient risk.

Roche reduced batch recall trace time from 72 hours to 4 hours after unifying QMS, MES, and ERP on Snowflake. Batch genealogy query spans every system in one GxP-governed query. FDA audit passed with Snowflake as system of record.

Regulatory Audit Finding Rate
Critical & Major Findings per FDA/EMA Audit
Target: 0 critical findings · <2 major/audit
FDA warning letters and consent decrees directly impact product supply and market value. Critical and major audit findings typically trace to data integrity failures: records that can't be reconstructed, audit trails that are incomplete, or data from multiple systems that contradict each other. GxP-compliant data governance is the root cause fix.

21 CFR Part 11-compliant audit trails, immutable lineage, and RBAC are native in Snowflake. Pre-inspection self-service query shifts audit prep from a 2-week data assembly sprint to hours.

Cold Chain & Serialisation Metrics

Cold Chain Excursion Rate
% of Shipments with Temperature Deviations
Industry: cold chain failures cost $35B/yr (WHO) Target: <0.5% of shipments with excursions
Cold chain excursions are among the highest-cost supply chain events in pharmaceutical. For biologics at 2–8°C, a single excursion destroys the batch. For cell therapies at −196°C, it may harm the patient. IoT sensor data from 3PL sites, aircraft, and distribution points generates millions of data points that existing systems cannot aggregate, let alone detect anomalies in.

Snowpipe streams IoT cold chain sensor data in real time into Snowflake. Cortex AI anomaly detection fires before excursion thresholds are crossed, enabling intervention before the batch is lost.

Serialisation Coverage %
% of Products with Complete DSCSA Serialisation
DSCSA: 100% serialisation compliance required
DSCSA (US) and EU FMD require every prescription drug unit to carry a unique identifier with a traceable chain of custody from manufacturer to pharmacy dispense. Compliance gaps are not a risk to manage — they are a barrier to market. Serialisation data typically lives in a purpose-built compliance system completely disconnected from QMS, ERP, and cold chain records.

DSCSA serialisation hub, QMS batch records, and cold chain data unified on Snowflake. Lot trace from manufacturer to pharmacy dispense in minutes, not hours. Cortex Analyst: "Show me all lots shipped to McKesson in the last 90 days with full chain of custody" in seconds.

GxP-governed semantic layer unifies serialisation, batch records, and distribution data. Lot trace drops from 48+ hours to under 4. Roche demonstrated this capability to the FDA and passed a consent decree inspection.

CATEGORY 03

Inventory & Working Capital Metrics

Days on Hand (DOH)
Average Inventory / Average Daily Usage
Branded pharma: 90–180 days · Generics: 60–120 days
Pharmaceutical DOH is high by design: regulatory-mandated safety stock, long manufacturing cycle times, and batch yield variability all force conservative inventory positioning. Reducing DOH without compromising patient supply requires precise demand sensing by channel. Every 10-day reduction in DOH on $500M inventory frees ~$14M in working capital.

IQVIA and hospital purchasing demand signals from Snowflake Marketplace improve demand accuracy, enabling DOH reduction without compromising patient supply SLAs.

Inventory Turns
COGS ÷ Average Inventory Value
Branded pharma: 2–4x · Generics: 4–8x
Lower than most industries due to mandated safety stock and long cycle times. Improving turns requires both better demand accuracy and shorter batch release cycle times. For pharma, inventory turns improvement is directly linked to batch right-first-time rate and CAPA cycle time.

Unified demand + batch release data enables better inventory positioning and faster batch-to-stock cycle, directly improving turns without compromising quality standards.

Safety Stock by Channel
Buffer Inventory Split by Clinical vs Commercial
Most pharma hold 30–50% more safety stock than demand risk requires
Pharmaceutical safety stock is sized against demand uncertainty that Snowflake can materially reduce. Clinical trial supply and commercial inventory are managed in entirely separate systems, causing chronic imbalances. Right-sizing requires channel-specific demand signals — hospital formulary, specialty pharmacy, and direct distribution each need separate models.

Cortex AI models channel-specific demand variability from IQVIA and hospital purchasing data. Hold more where clinical trial supply is uncertain; hold less where commercial demand is predictable. Safety stock right-sized to actual risk.

Near-Expiry Stock %
Inventory Within 12 Months of Shelf-Life Expiry
Industry avg: 8–15% of inventory near-expiry Target: <5%
Near-expiry pharmaceutical inventory has limited recovery options: expedited channel promotion, donation, or write-off. Unlike CPG, there is no mark-down pricing for branded pharma. Write-offs are P&L events. Root cause is almost always inaccurate demand forecasting by channel, combined with late visibility into batch expiry dates across multiple warehouses and 3PLs.

Cortex AI detects approaching expiry risk by product-location-channel combination before write-offs materialise. Unified WMS and ERP data identifies transfer or expedite opportunities to consume at-risk stock before expiry.

API Supply Buffer Days
Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient Days of Coverage
Target: 90–180 days API buffer for critical products
API supply is the critical path for pharmaceutical manufacturing. API shortages, particularly from single-source suppliers in Asia, create batch schedule failures that cannot be recovered within the planning horizon. Visibility into API supplier capacity, quality status, and lead time requires connecting procurement, QMS, and supply planning data.

Snowflake Marketplace delivers supplier risk signals, geopolitical indicators, and port/logistics data. Cortex AI scores API supply risk 6–8 weeks before production impact, enabling proactive sourcing rather than emergency response.

Cash-to-Cash Cycle Time
DIO + DSO − DPO
Branded pharma: 120–200 days Target: reduce 10–20%
The CFO's primary pharmaceutical supply chain metric. Long manufacturing cycles, batch release hold times, and distribution complexity make pharma cash conversion inherently long. A 20-day improvement on a $2B pharma business frees ~$110M in working capital. Better demand accuracy and faster batch release are the two levers most directly connected to Snowflake.

Snowflake connects supply chain and financial data in one GxP-governed platform. Batch release cycle time reduction and DOH optimisation both flow directly to cash-to-cash improvement, with full financial traceability.

Manufacturing Excellence Metrics

OEE
Overall Equipment Effectiveness
Pharma avg: 55–75% World-class: 85%+
In pharmaceutical batch manufacturing, OEE loss comes from equipment changeovers, cleaning validation, unplanned stoppages from QC hold decisions, and yield losses from in-process deviation. Every 1% OEE improvement on a high-volume injectable line adds capacity without capex. Detecting OEE degradation early requires correlating SCADA, MES, and QMS data in real time.

Cortex AI detects OEE degradation patterns in MES and SCADA data streams in real time. Anomaly detected before it impacts batch yield. Unified data enables root cause correlation to ingredient lots, process parameters, or equipment cycles.

Batch Cycle Time
Time from Batch Start to Batch Release
Industry avg: 14–90 days (depends on modality) Target: reduce 20% via data-driven QC
Batch cycle time determines how much working capital is tied up in WIP and how quickly supply can respond to demand changes. Quality hold times are the primary driver of long cycles, and most holds are caused by incomplete in-process data rather than genuine product risk. Reducing cycle time requires connecting QC test results in real time to batch status decisions.

Snowflake unifies LIMS test results, MES batch records, and QMS status in real time. QC hold decisions accelerate when all data is available immediately, not when it arrives from the slowest system.

Deviation Rate
Deviations per 1,000 Batch Records
Industry avg: varies widely by complexity Target: continuous reduction via AI trend detection
Pharmaceutical deviations are compliance events with direct cost: investigation time, CAPA, potential batch rejection, and regulatory reporting for critical deviations. Root cause analysis across QMS, MES, and LIMS is the bottleneck. Systemic deviation patterns (repeat deviations from the same root cause) are identifiable only when data from all systems is unified.

Cortex AI identifies repeat deviation patterns across batches, products, and sites. Systemic root cause visible in Snowflake before the deviation is filed, enabling preventive action rather than repeated corrective cycles.

Schedule Attainment %
Actual Production vs Planned Schedule
Industry avg: 75–88% Target: 95%+
In pharmaceutical, schedule attainment failures cascade: a missed batch creates a supply gap that often cannot be recovered within the demand planning horizon, because batch cycle times are weeks not days. Root cause is almost always a data disconnect: API availability, equipment status, and demand signal changes not reaching the production scheduler in time.

Live API availability, equipment maintenance schedules, and demand signals unified in one Snowflake query. Cortex AI flags schedule risk before the batch is cancelled, not after the supply gap opens.

Batch Release Cycle Time
QC Testing Completion to Final Product Release
Industry avg: 7–28 days for biologics Target: reduce 30% via data availability
Batch release cycle time directly determines revenue timing and DOH. Most release delays are caused by data availability, not product risk: lab results in LIMS not synchronised with QMS, certificate of analysis assembly requiring manual extraction, or reviewer queues caused by data completeness issues. All are data infrastructure problems.

LIMS, QMS, and ERP data unified on Snowflake means reviewers have all batch record data available immediately. Cortex Analyst enables "Show me all in-process QC results for Batch X" in seconds, from a GxP-auditable source.

Capacity Utilisation
Actual Output vs Rated Batch Capacity
Target: 70–80% sustainable for pharma batch lines
Pharmaceutical capacity planning is complicated by modality constraints (dedicated suites for biologics, aseptic fill-finish bottlenecks), variable batch cycle times, and clinical trial supply requirements competing with commercial demand. Optimising across clinical and commercial on constrained assets requires unified demand forecast and batch scheduling data.

Snowflake unifies clinical and commercial demand plans with MES capacity data and batch scheduling constraints. Cortex AI enables scenario modelling: "Can we accommodate the clinical supply request if we reschedule these commercial batches?" answered in seconds.
