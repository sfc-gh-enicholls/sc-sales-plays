Discovery Questions
Opener: “How many systems does your team reconcile before making a supply planning decision today?”

VP Manufacturing / Head of Ops
Discovery Questions
Opener: “What’s the cost of an hour of unplanned downtime on your highest-volume production line?”
“When an ECO is issued, how long does it take for MES, ERP, and procurement to all reflect the change?”
“If your production planner wants to know which components are below reorder for next week’s schedule, how long does it take to get that answer?”
CIO / CDO / VP Data
Discovery Questions
Opener: “Of the AI or ML pilots you’ve run in manufacturing, how many have made it into production?”
“If your CSCO asks a supply chain question, how long before they get a trusted answer from your data team?”
“Do your engineering, procurement, and production teams work from the same definition of OEE and cycle time — or do they argue about numbers in S&OP?”
CPO / Head of Supply Planning
Discovery Questions
Opener: “How far in advance do you typically know when a component from a sole-source supplier is at risk?”
“What percentage of your supply planning team’s time is spent preparing data before S&OP vs actually making decisions in it?”
“When a supplier misses a delivery, how quickly can your team execute the response across ERP, scheduling, and procurement?”

Objection Handling
Common Objections & Tight Responses
“We’re already committed to [SAP IBP / Kinaxis / o9] for planning.”
Snowflake is not a replacement for your ERP, MES, or planning tool — it sits on top of them. ABB and Honeywell run SAP and Snowflake side by side.
“IT won’t support another platform to integrate and maintain.”
Zero-copy means no new pipelines for IT to build or maintain. Your systems stay exactly where they are.
“We already have a data warehouse / data lake.”
Snowflake likely works alongside it, not instead of it. The question is whether your manufacturing systems and AI actually trust what’s in it.
“We’re not ready for AI yet.”
That’s exactly why we start with the data foundation. Most aren’t — the data fragmentation is the reason AI hasn’t worked yet.
“Budget is tight — this isn’t a priority right now.”
Forrester found <6 month payback. ABB found $200M+ annual savings. Supply chain is typically 50–70% of revenue — even a 1% improvement is material.
“We’re evaluating alternatives that do everything in one platform.”
Closed, proprietary platforms lock your data in. Snowflake is open — your stack stays yours. The world’s best supply chain apps (including Blue Yonder) chose Snowflake as their data foundation.
