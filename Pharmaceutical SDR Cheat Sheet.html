Objection Handling
Common Objections & Responses
“We’re already committed to [Veeva Vault / Oracle HLS / SAP QM] for quality management.”
Snowflake connects to your QMS, not replaces it. Veeva Vault and SAP QM keep running your quality processes. Snowflake joins QMS with MES, LIMS, and ERP so you can see the complete batch record in one query without touching your existing systems.
“IT won’t support another platform to integrate and maintain.”
Zero-copy means no new pipelines for IT to build or maintain. Your systems stay exactly where they are. GxP governance is built into Snowflake, not a layer IT has to implement.
“We already have a data warehouse / data lake.”
Snowflake likely works alongside it. The question is whether your QMS, MES, and LIMS data in that warehouse has the lineage and audit trail to pass an FDA inspection. Most don’t.
“We’re not sure GxP-compliant AI is possible.”
Snowflake has 21 CFR Part 11-compliant audit trails, RBAC, and data lineage built in. Every AI answer traces back to the source row, which is exactly what a GxP validation requires. Roche passed an FDA audit with Snowflake as the system of record.
“Budget is tight, this isn’t a priority right now.”
A pharmaceutical product recall averages $100M+ in direct costs. One avoided cold chain batch failure = $400K+. DSCSA non-compliance = potential market withdrawal. The regulatory obligations are the business case — you don’t have to build it.
“We’re evaluating alternatives that do GxP in one platform.”
A closed platform reaches a ceiling at QMS. It can’t ingest MES SCADA streams, cold chain IoT, or IQVIA prescribing data. Snowflake connects, extends, then lets you rationalise what you no longer need, on your timetable.
