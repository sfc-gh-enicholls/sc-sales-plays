CATEGORY 01

Customer Service & Fill Performance

Case Fill Rate
% of Cases Shipped vs Ordered
Industry avg: 94–96% Target: 98.5%+
The primary customer service KPI for CPG. Every percentage point below target triggers retailer deductions, chargebacks, or loss of shelf space. For CPG companies with Walmart, Amazon, or Kroger as customers, fill rate directly determines profitability. A 1-point improvement on $2B of orders eliminates ~$20M in chargebacks and lost sales.

Snowflake unifies ERP, WMS, and demand plan in real time. Cortex Analyst enables "what is case fill rate for customer X this week?" in seconds — answered from live data, not last night's batch.

OTIF
On Time In Full — Retailer Compliance
Walmart target: 98%+ Penalty per failure: 3% of invoice
Retailer compliance performance that directly affects invoice value. Walmart charges a 3% deduction for every OTIF failure; Amazon uses SCI to penalise non-compliant suppliers. For a CPG company with $500M in Walmart sales, every 1% OTIF miss is $1.5M in deductions. Root cause is almost always a demand signal that didn't reach supply planning in time.

Live demand sensing on Snowflake detects fill risk 4–6 weeks before retailer order fulfillment. Cortex AI flags at-risk SKU-location combinations so teams act before the chargeback, not after.

On-Shelf Availability
% of Time Product Is Available at Shelf
Industry avg: 95% Best-in-class: 98%+
The revenue metric retailers care about most. Every point of OOS is a sale lost — to a competitor, to private label, or to the retailer's own brand. OSA is driven by case fill rate and DC replenishment, but its root cause is almost always upstream in demand planning accuracy.

Retailer POS and syndicated data (NielsenIQ, Circana) unified with internal supply data on Snowflake. Cortex AI detects OSA deterioration by SKU-store before replenishment orders are placed.

Perfect Order Rate
Complete · On Time · Correct · Undamaged
Industry avg: 85–92%
Composite measure of order completeness, delivery timing, documentation accuracy, and product condition. A single failure in any dimension counts as an imperfect order. Drives retailer relationship quality and deduction frequency in complex CPG supply chains.

Unified data layer correlates WMS shipment data, ERP order records, and logistics events to automatically calculate perfect order rate — no manual cross-system reconciliation.

Retailer SCI Score
Amazon Supplier Confirmation Index
Target: 95%+ to avoid velocity restrictions
Amazon's measure of supplier reliability — confirmation rate, cancellation rate, and fill rate combined. SCI score below threshold triggers Amazon to reduce purchase order volume from that supplier and route demand to competitors. Recovery from a poor SCI score takes 90+ days. Prevention requires upstream demand signal clarity.

Cortex AI monitors Amazon order confirmation and fill signals in near real-time. Exception alerts surface SKU-level risk to the supply planning team before confirmation windows close.

Forecast Bias
Systematic Over or Under-Prediction
Target: ±5% at product family · ±10% at SKU
Persistent forecast bias distorts inventory positioning across the entire supply chain. In CPG, over-forecasting drives waste and markdowns; under-forecasting drives OOS and chargebacks. Promotional forecast bias is the most common and most damaging — post-event demand voids are the direct result.

Cortex AI detects systematic bias by SKU, category, and customer — automatically correcting model inputs faster than any monthly planning cycle can.

Kraft Heinz: out-of-stocks reduced from 20% to 6% in 4 weeks on Snowflake  ·  Keurig Dr Pepper: $14M in self-service analytics value across SC and commercial teams (⚠ internal use only — do not share externally)

CATEGORY 02

Demand Planning & Promotional Intelligence

Forecast Accuracy (MAPE/WMAPE)
Mean Absolute Percentage Error
SKU level: avg 30–45% MAPE Target: <15% MAPE
The foundation metric for everything downstream. A 10-point MAPE improvement on a CPG company with $1B revenue reduces excess inventory, emergency freight, and lost sales by tens of millions. Most CPG AI demand sensing pilots fail because retailer POS data is fragmented and not unified with internal planning data.

Cortex AI demand models retrain continuously on live signals — order book, POS shipment history, and external market data from Snowflake Marketplace (consumer sentiment, economic indicators). Forecast accuracy compounds over time.

Promotional Lift Accuracy
Predicted vs Actual Promotional Uplift
Industry avg error: 25–40% on incremental volume Target: <15% error
Trade promotions represent 15–25% of CPG revenue. A 10% error in promotional lift forecast creates a wave of either excess inventory or OOS across every promotional cycle — amplified across the full SKU range. Root cause: promotion plans are built in TPM without live supply constraints, and historical lift data is never systematically unified with demand baselines.

Snowflake unifies TPM promotion calendars, retailer POS sell-through, historical base and incremental lift, and supply constraints into one governed platform. Cortex AI builds promotional response models from every historical event — improving lift accuracy with every promotion run.

Trade Promotion ROI
Revenue Return on Trade Spend Investment
50–70% of CPG promotions generate negative ROI (McKinsey)
Trade spend is the second largest line item in the P&L after COGS for most CPG companies. The tragedy: most promotions cannot be evaluated post-event because sell-through data from retailers, base volume, and incremental lift are never unified in one system. Decisions are based on intuition, not evidence.

Retailer POS sell-through unified with TPM spend, base volume baselines, and financial actuals on Snowflake. Cortex Analyst enables post-promotion review in seconds: "What was the incremental ROI of our Q2 Walmart promotion on SKU X?" — answered from live data.

NPD Launch Forecast
Forecast Accuracy for NPD in First 3 Months
Average NPD fill rate in week 1: <70% Target: 90%+
New product launches are the highest-stakes demand forecasting event in CPG. Getting it wrong in either direction costs: OOS in week 1 loses distribution; overstock creates delistings. Root cause: there's no historical data for genuinely new products, so analogues must be selected — a process that requires unified data across all SKUs, all customers, all categories.

Cortex AI enables analogue-based NPD forecasting: "Which existing SKU most closely matches this new product's launch profile?" — answered instantly from unified historical data. Launch fill rate confidence improves materially.

S&OP Cycle Time
S&OP Pack Prep to Decision

In most CPG companies, the S&OP meeting is the most expensive reconciliation exercise in the business. Demand, supply, and finance arrive with different numbers from different systems. The first hour is spent agreeing on the facts, not making decisions. Root cause: no shared data platform where all functions see the same demand signal.

Snowflake's governed semantic layer ensures every function reads from the same defined KPIs. Cortex Analyst generates S&OP pack data on demand — "show me the demand gap for next quarter by category" answered in seconds.

CATEGORY 03

Inventory & Working Capital

Days of Supply
Inventory on Hand ÷ Average Daily Sales
Food & Bev: 25–45 days · Personal Care: 35–60 days
Direct measure of how much capital is locked in inventory. For CPG with seasonal products and promotional cycles, DoS fluctuates dramatically — the challenge is keeping DoS right-sized despite volatile promotional demand. Every 5-day reduction in DoS on $500M inventory frees ~$7M in working capital.

Cortex AI-driven demand sensing and live TPM signals reduce the uncertainty that forces excess DoS. Governed semantic layer means finance, supply, and operations agree on the same inventory number — no padding for reconciliation uncertainty.

Inventory Turns
COGS ÷ Average Inventory Value
Food & Bev: 8–15x · Personal Care: 5–10x · Household: 5–8x
Higher turns = less capital tied up, less obsolescence risk, lower carrying costs. CPG inventory turns are highly sensitive to promotional forecasting accuracy. A turns improvement of 1x on a $300M inventory base frees ~$30M in working capital — directly improving cash-to-cash cycle.

Unified demand + promotional signals + supply data drives better turns through more accurate inventory positioning by SKU, DC, and customer.

Safety Stock Level
Buffer Inventory Held Against Forecast Uncertainty
Most CPG hold 20–35% more safety stock than demand risk requires
Safety stock in CPG is rational insurance against demand and supply variability — the problem is when it's sized to cover data uncertainty rather than genuine business risk. Most CPG companies hold significantly more safety stock than necessary because they don't trust their promotional forecasts or supply signals.

Cortex AI continuous risk scoring enables right-sizing safety stock by actual demand variability — not as a blanket hedge against data lag. Hold less where promotional forecasts are reliable; hold more where NPD uncertainty is genuine.

Excess & Obsolete Inventory
E&O as % of Total Inventory Value
Industry avg: 5–12% Best-in-class: <2%
E&O in CPG is driven by promotional over-forecasting, failed NPD launches, and poor visibility into aging stock near shelf-life expiry. An average $1B CPG company writes off $25–60M annually in E&O. Short-dated stock near expiry is particularly costly — markdown, donation, or disposal all destroy value.

Cortex AI detects aging inventory and near-expiry risk before write-offs materialise. Unified WMS and demand data identifies inter-DC transfer opportunities to consume at-risk stock before expiry.

Cash-to-Cash Cycle Time
DIO + DSO − DPO
Industry avg: 30–60 days Target: reduce 10–20%
The CFO's primary supply chain metric. Every 10-day improvement on a $2B CPG business frees ~$55M in cash. CPG cash conversion is uniquely affected by promotional timing — large retailer orders at quarter-end, followed by voids, create working capital peaks and troughs that better demand planning can smooth.

Snowflake connects supply chain and financial data in one platform — enabling real-time cash-to-cash tracking and scenario modelling for working capital optimisation decisions.

Inventory Carrying Cost
25–35% of Annual Inventory Value
Carrying cost rate: 25–35%/yr
The true cost of holding inventory: capital cost + storage + handling + insurance + write-off risk. A CPG company with $400M in average inventory pays $100–140M per year just to hold it. Even a 10% inventory reduction saves $10–14M annually — before counting the freed working capital.

Better demand/supply matching through unified Snowflake data and Cortex AI directly reduces average inventory held. ABB (Discreet Mfg) found $200M+ in annual savings — the principle applies equally to CPG inventory reduction.

CATEGORY 04

Operations & Manufacturing Performance

OEE
Overall Equipment Effectiveness
Industry avg: 65% World-class: 85%+
The composite production efficiency metric: Availability × Performance × Quality. In CPG process manufacturing (food, beverage, personal care), OEE loss comes from changeovers between SKUs, cleaning in place (CIP), and unplanned line stoppages from ingredient shortages. Every 1% OEE improvement on a high-volume line adds capacity without capex.

Cortex AI detects OEE degradation patterns in MES and SCADA data before they cause stoppages. Unified ingredient availability and production schedule data reduces changeover frequency by enabling optimised run sequencing.

Schedule Adherence
Actual Production vs Plan
Industry avg: 80–88% Target: 95%+
The direct measure of whether manufacturing is executing to the promotional and replenishment plan. Low schedule adherence drives premium freight, OOS at retail, and retailer chargebacks. Root cause is almost always upstream: stale demand signal causing a plan that couldn't be executed against available ingredients.

Live ingredient availability from Snowflake feeds into production planning — schedules built on real data. Cortex AI flags ingredient shortages before the schedule is locked, not after the line stops.

Waste & Loss %
Ingredient and Finished Goods Waste as % of Output
Industry avg: 3–8% Target: <2%
CPG process manufacturing generates significant waste through product changeovers, CIP loss, and shelf-life-driven write-offs. Waste directly impacts COGS and sustainability targets. Root cause analysis requires correlating production data with ingredient batch records and yield models — typically fragmented across QMS, MES, and ERP.

Unified QMS, MES, and ingredient data on Snowflake enables automatic correlation of waste events with ingredient batches, process parameters, and changeover sequences. AI anomaly detection triggers before waste accumulates.

Batch Yield
Actual Output vs Theoretical Yield
Target: 98%+ for established products
In CPG process manufacturing, batch yield directly drives COGS. A 1% yield improvement on a product with 60% gross margin and $200M revenue is worth ~$1.2M annually. Yield variation is often linked to specific ingredient supplier lots, seasonal raw material quality, or undetected process parameter drift.

Cortex AI connects batch yield records with ingredient lot traceability, supplier data, and environmental parameters. Root cause of yield degradation identified in minutes — before it affects the full production run.

Capacity Utilisation
Actual Output vs Rated Capacity
Target: 75–85% sustainable
Running too low wastes capital investment. Running too high creates quality risk and no buffer for demand surges. CPG capacity utilisation is complicated by SKU proliferation and promotional demand spikes — optimising across 500+ SKUs on 20+ lines requires unified demand forecast and production scheduling data.

Snowflake unifies MES capacity data, production schedules, and demand plans in one query. Cortex AI enables scenario modelling for promotional capacity allocation — "can we accommodate this promotion if we reschedule line 3?" answered in seconds.

Changeover Frequency
Number of Product Changeovers per Day/Week
Varies by line complexity
In CPG process manufacturing, changeovers represent the single largest source of OEE loss — typically 30–40% of all downtime. Reducing changeover frequency through better run sequencing directly improves OEE, yield, and cleaning time. Optimal sequencing requires unified demand forecast + production schedule + product compatibility data.

Unified demand forecast and production compatibility data on Snowflake enables AI-optimised run sequencing — minimising changeovers while meeting fill rate targets. Cortex Analyst: "What's the optimal production sequence for next week's demand?" answered in seconds.

Revenue & Financial Impact

Net Revenue per Case
Revenue after Trade Deductions
Industry: highly variable by category
The real revenue metric. Gross revenue minus deductions (OTIF penalties, promotional allowances, off-invoice discounts) = net revenue. Every OTIF improvement and chargeback reduction flows directly to net revenue. Trade spend is the primary lever — but only if promotional ROI is measurable.

Unified TPM, deduction management, and financial data means every trade decision has a real net revenue impact. Cortex Analyst: "What is our net revenue per case for customer X after all deductions?" answered from live data.

Trade Spend as % Revenue
Total Trade Investment ÷ Gross Revenue
CPG average: 15–25% of revenue
For many CPG companies, trade spend is the second largest P&L line after COGS. Despite this scale, most companies cannot measure whether individual promotions make money. Snowflake unifies retailer sell-through with TPM spend data — making every promotion measurable and every future investment decision evidence-based.

Cortex Analyst delivers post-promotion analysis in seconds: incremental lift vs. base, net revenue impact, and comparison to similar historical events. Trade decisions become data-driven rather than relationship-driven.

Snowflake customers report 1–3 point gross margin improvement from combined inventory, waste, and logistics efficiencies.
