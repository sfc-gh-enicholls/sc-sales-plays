The industry challenge

Tomorrow's delivery. Today's shelf life.

More stock can improve availability and create tomorrow's waste. Daily replenishment needs to see what will sell before expiry, what is actually on the shelf and what will arrive next.

Make availability and waste one decision, not two competing reports.
