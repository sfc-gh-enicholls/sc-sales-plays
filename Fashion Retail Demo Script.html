Signal to Shelf Conceptual Demo Flow

From Signal to Shelf — Fashion Retail scenario — EMEA Fashion & Apparel

What we’re doing & how it relates to the play

“Stop finding out a style is dying on the shelf the same week you're forced into a deep markdown.”

This demo is the conceptual centrepiece for the “Signal to Shelf” Fashion Retail play. The play’s premise: across EMEA fashion retail, every major trading theme — markdown pressure, terminal stock, trend-chasing, and CSRD/ESPR compliance — is fundamentally a data problem. Fragmented systems force buying and planning teams to react to sell-through after the fact; Snowflake collapses that into one governed platform so they see it live and act early.

Positioning: Signal to Shelf helps EMEA fashion retail leaders turn fragmented trading data into a single, trusted intelligence layer — so every decision, from chase to markdown to compliance, is made with days of runway, not hindsight.

What this demo proves: one narrative event — a style unexpectedly trending on social media (chase opportunity) colliding with a core-range style falling behind plan (markdown risk) and a vendor capacity constraint (chase risk) — followed across four personas, all on Snowflake. It maps to the play’s arc: Signal (AI surfaces live sell-through and flags the trend before it's obvious — S1–2) → Shelf (AI quantifies the markdown and chase decisions, runs optimised scenarios — S1, 3–4) → Proof (bridge: same platform underpins CSRD/ESPR traceability — not demonstrated below; add a closing slide).

Fictional Company — Demo Context
Company
Fictional mid-market EMEA fashion retailer
Multi-channel apparel business trading across owned stores, e-commerce, and select wholesale.
Trading Model
OTB committed months ahead of season, seasonal buying cycles, vendor lead times, and a live e-commerce channel that surfaces trend signal early.
Planning Context
Trend volatility, size-curve complexity, and in-season chase windows create persistent markdown and terminal stock risk.

The Narrative Event — Multiple Pressures Converging

Demand upside: A style has started trending on social media, creating a short window to chase stock and capture a season-defining sales spike
Markdown risk: A core-range style is running well behind plan, heading toward heavy terminal stock at season end if nothing changes
Supply risk: The vendor for the trending style has limited spare capacity, creating a race against the chase window before the trend cools

Section 1 — Trading Health & Alerts

Persona: Chief Trading Officer
Scene 1a · Trading Health Overview
Setup
The Chief Trading Officer starts the trading meeting in a single control tower dashboard — no more pulling exports from store POS, e-commerce, and planning separately.
What to show
  • Control tower with at-a-glance KPIs: full-price sell-through, OTB variance, terminal stock risk
  • Real-time AI insights surfacing the styles that need a decision today
  • Customizable alert thresholds (only the styles that matter show up)
Aha moment
Call out: AI-driven dashboard eliminates manual export reconciliation. The Chief Trading Officer isn’t waiting for Monday's report — they're managing by exception, today.
So what
Outcome: Proactive vs. reactive trading management. Hours saved on report assembly, redirected to decisions.
Scene 1b · Key Alerts
Setup
The system has flagged two critical developments overnight:
What to show
  • AI-powered alert feed, personalized and prioritized by margin impact
  • System auto-quantifies risk in financial terms: “£XXk of margin at risk if this style isn't marked down within 2 weeks”
  • Instant visibility into the financial consequence of inaction on both the chase opportunity and the markdown risk
Aha moment
Call out: The system doesn’t just flag styles — it puts pound values on the decision so the trading team can prioritise fast.
So what
Outcome: Leadership is always informed of trading risk and understands the margin impact before it compounds into deep clearance.
Scene 1c · Scenario Comparison
Setup
Given the chase opportunity and markdown risk, the Chief Trading Officer wants to evaluate options quickly.
What to show
  • Copy from base trading plan, modify parameters, add comments — collaborative workflow creation
  • Automated workflow assigns tasks to buying, planning, and finance
  • Side-by-side scenario comparison on key metrics: revenue, margin, terminal stock impact
Aha moment
Call out: Workflow creation is automated and linked to scenario creation — no manual task assignment, no delays. Comments route to the right owner immediately.
So what
Outcome: Faster decisions, better decisions — full margin impact visibility, cross-functional alignment built into the workflow.

Section 2 — Sell-Through & Chase Opportunity

Persona: Head of Planning
Scene 2a · Live Sell-Through Reforecast
Setup
The Head of Planning needs to keep the in-season reforecast current without drowning in manual exports from POS and e-commerce.
What to show
  • Live sell-through dashboard by style, size, and channel with “auto-selected” best fit trend model highlighted
  • Reforecast contribution metric — highlights where planner input improves accuracy vs. hurts it
  • Focuses planner time on the styles that actually need judgement
Aha moment
Call out: AI continuously updates the sell-through reforecast. The planner only intervenes where they add value, as measured against the model. It prevents interventions that reduce accuracy.
So what
Outcome: Higher reforecast accuracy with less manual effort. Planner time freed for scenario modelling and chase decisions.
Scene 2b · Chase Opportunity Review
Setup
The trending style creates a new chase scenario to model. The planner needs to quantify volume and margin impact of chasing more stock quickly.
What to show
  • Scenario planning dashboard with real-time margin impact visualisation
  • AI agent surfaces relevant historical trend scenarios with related assumptions for the planner to build from
  • Planner tweaks chase parameters (quantity, timing, size curve, vendor)
  • Comparison chart: revenue/margin impact vs. current OTB targets
  • Immediate cross-functional task assignment to buying and finance
Aha moment
Call out: The AI agent enhances scenario modelling with insights from past trend events. Immediate revenue/margin visibility into the chase decision.
So what
Outcome: Early identification of the trend enables an evidence-backed chase decision while there's still season left to sell it. Enables cross-functional alignment with buying and finance before the order is placed.

Section 3 — Markdown & Terminal Stock Risk

Persona: Head of Buying
Scene 3a · Markdown Risk Review
Setup
The system has already identified the underperforming core-range style (feeding from the alerts in Section 1). Two specific risk drivers are surfaced:
What to show
  • Solver re-runs automatically across store and channel sell-through for two scenarios (early shallow markdown vs. wait and see)
  • Markdown risk dashboard showing projected terminal stock over time with respective drivers by store
  • Initiative tracking: mitigation actions, their margin impact, and ROI for management decisions
  • AI-powered recommendations and drill-down ranking for markdown depth and timing
Aha moment
Call out: AI-driven optimisation reduces manual effort. Early risk identification provides more runway to act with a shallower markdown. The system prioritises and recommends drill-down actions to minimise terminal stock and maximise recovered margin.
So what
Outcome: Minimises terminal stock and deep clearance. Accelerates decision-making and enables an early, shallow markdown instead of a late, deep one.
Scene 3b · Store-Level Drill-Down
Setup
The underperformance is concentrated in specific stores and regions. The Head of Buying drills into this from the markdown risk overview.
What to show
  • Store-level drill-down dashboard: sell-through by location, inventory position, local promotional activity
  • Buyer identifies that inter-store transfer to higher-performing locations resolves some of the excess (requires management sign-off with trade-off evaluation)
  • ROI/cost/investment impact evaluations for trade-offs: transfer cost vs. margin recovered vs. markdown avoided
  • Targeted local markdown bridges the remaining risk at a shallower discount than a blanket markdown
  • Both actions auto-documented as drafts, triggering action owners to review and financially evaluate
Aha moment
Call out: Store-level visibility is automated — real-time monitoring. Decision trade-offs are quantified for management.
So what
Outcome: Maximises margin recovery by reallocating stock to where it will sell. Aligns store-level decisions with strategic and financial trade-offs. Ensures company-wide optimisation.

Section 4 — Vendor Chase Sourcing

Persona: Head of Buying
Scene 4a · Vendor Capacity Drill-Down
Setup
The vendor for the trending style has limited spare capacity, threatening the chase window. Cascading effects are expected on delivery timing over the coming weeks. The buyer analyses volume at risk and defines mitigation actions:
What to show
  • Sourcing drill-down dashboard: primary vendor constraints, at-risk chase order volumes
  • Vendor performance scorecard comparing primary and backup vendors on OTIF, capacity, and compliance
  • Financial trade-off comparison: cost of splitting the chase order across two vendors vs. delay risk from a single vendor
Aha moment
Call out: Proactive vendor risk detection enables early mitigation. Scenario modelling quantifies the financial impact of splitting the order to protect the chase window.
So what
Outcome: Enhances chase reliability through improved vendor visibility and proactive risk management. Protects the trend window by securing supply ahead of potential delay.
Scene 4b · Trading Plan Review (Updated)
Setup
The Chief Trading Officer reviews all newly created mitigation initiatives, categorised by scenario. Each scenario outlines specific actions from the chase, markdown, and vendor analyses.
What to show
  • Trading plan dashboard with prioritised initiatives per scenario
  • Side-by-side scenario comparison: margin and revenue impact
  • Approval workflow module for streamlined management decision-making
  • All chase and markdown actions aggregated into structured, actionable initiatives
Aha moment
Call out: Seamless transition to executive decision-making. All actions aggregated and ready for the trading meeting.
So what
Outcome: Provides a structured approach for resolving both the chase and the markdown, ensuring cross-team alignment. Enables data-driven trading decisions, optimising margin across the full range.
