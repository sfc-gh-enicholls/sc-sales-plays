What is Consumer Packaged Goods?

The manufacturing and distribution of branded, frequently purchased consumer products sold through retailers. CPG companies use process manufacturing (batches, recipes, formulations) and distribute through major retailers — making demand forecasting, promotional planning, and retailer compliance the central supply chain challenges.
