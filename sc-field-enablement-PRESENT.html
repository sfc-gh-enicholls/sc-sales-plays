2020 — Toyota detects the shortage 8 weeks early. Builds buffer stock.

Every line keeps running. $20B+ competitive advantage.

The difference wasn't the shortage. Every automaker faced the same shortage. Toyota had the data visibility to see it coming. Everyone else found out when the truck didn't arrive.
