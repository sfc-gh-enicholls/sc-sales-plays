What is Discrete Manufacturing?

The production of distinct, countable, individually identifiable units assembled from a Bill of Materials. Unlike process manufacturing (chemicals, food), discrete manufacturers build serialised or lot-tracked products — think an engine, a circuit board, or a CT scanner — where each unit can be tracked, reworked, or recalled individually. Supply chains are BOM-driven, multi-tier, and highly sensitive to component availability and engineering change.
