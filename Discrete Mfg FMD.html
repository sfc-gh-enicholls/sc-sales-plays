OUR STARTING POINT

Supply Chain Stopped Being a Cost Centre. It Became the Risk Register.

Four things now sit in your remit that used to sit somewhere else, or nowhere at all. Each one moved because the board needed someone accountable for it, and supply chain was the only function with line of sight across plants, suppliers, and OEM commitments.

Growth
You decide what the plant network can credibly commit to

An OEM can accelerate a platform ramp. Only supply can say whether capacity and components can be secured in time, and what it costs to get there.

Cash
You hold the largest lever on the balance sheet

Inventory and safety stock are typically the largest working capital line in a discrete manufacturer. When the CFO needs cash without touching price or headcount, the conversation comes to you.

Resilience
You are the function the board asks about tier-3 exposure

Tariffs, export controls, single-source materials, geopolitics. Continuity moved from an operational concern to a standing board item, and most of the risk sits two or three tiers away from any system you can see.

Regulatory
You carry statutory obligations with dates attached

Conflict minerals due diligence, CBAM, EUDR, and customer-mandated serialisation are law, not policy. They are multi-tier traceability problems, which makes them supply chain problems, which makes them yours.

What changed

The mandate. Supply chain went from executing the plan to being asked what happens three tiers upstream, and how fast you can prove it.

What didn’t

Your visibility. You can see your own BOM and your tier-1 suppliers. Almost nobody can see further than that, and closing that gap is the subject of the rest of this conversation.

HOW

Four Things Make That Possible. None of Them Move Your Systems.

You have four reasonable questions about what you just saw. Each has a specific answer, and the four answers together are the platform.

01
Zero-Copy Integration

“IT hasn’t got the bandwidth for another integration programme.”

Your ERP, MES, PLM, and QMS are read where they already sit. No pipelines to build, no ETL to maintain. Connectivity is measured in weeks, and it is not a migration.

02
Multi-Tier Data Sharing

“Our suppliers will never give us that visibility, and we can’t force our own suppliers to disclose theirs.”

Nobody discloses a full supplier list. Each tier shares only the specific, scoped exposure data the next tier needs — live, governed, and without copying a file or granting blanket access. This is the capability no incumbent planning tool has.

03
Semantic Layer

“Our numbers won’t agree. They never do.”

OTIF, OEE, first pass yield, and component risk exposure are defined once, and every query, dashboard, and agent inherits that definition. Production and procurement stop reconciling and start deciding.

04
Cortex AI

“Our data can’t leave our control, and I can’t defend an AI answer I can’t trace.”

Models run inside your account, inside your RBAC, masking and lineage. Nothing is copied out. Every answer traces back to the row it came from, including the shared row from a tier-2 partner.

Each one is a prerequisite for the decision cycle you just saw. All four are native to the platform, not integrations you assemble. And not one of them requires a supplier onboarding programme before you can start.
