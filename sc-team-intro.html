FOUNDATIONS · SETTING THE SCENE

Supply chain is everything it takes to get a product from raw material to customer

📋
PLAN

Forecast demand. Set inventory targets. Decide what to make and when.

🏭
SOURCE

Buy raw materials and components. Manage supplier relationships and lead times.

⚙️
MAKE

Production planning, scheduling, manufacturing execution. Shop floor to finished goods.

🚚
DELIVER

Warehousing, logistics, last-mile. Getting the right product to the right place at the right time.

↩️
RETURN

Reverse logistics, warranties, recalls. Often completely disconnected from the rest.

The data reality
✗Each step runs on a different system — ERP, WMS, TMS, APS, POS
✗None of them share data in real time
✗Planning decisions made on data that's days or weeks stale
✗When something breaks, it takes 18 hours to find out
What this means in a manufacturing account
→The planning team runs S&OP off 5 spreadsheets exported from 3 systems
→Finance and Supply always argue because they have different numbers
→They bought an AI planning tool — it's underperforming because the data is wrong
→That broken data foundation is Snowflake's entry point.

Supply chain is Plan → Source → Make → Deliver → Return. The data connecting those steps is broken. That's our opportunity.

THE PERSONAS · WHO TO CALL ON

Three layers, five buyers, one data problem

Executive layer — economic buyer + strategic sponsor
CSCO / VP Supply Chain

P&L owner for SC. Accountable for service levels, cost-to-serve, resilience.

Language: resilience, OTIF, margin
CPO / Chief Procurement Officer

Owns supplier relationships and raw material cost. Urgently needs tier-N risk visibility.

Language: supplier risk, spend analytics
CFO (SC angle)

Cares about working capital, inventory carrying cost, cash conversion cycle.

Language: inventory turns, cash, WC
Operations layer — technical champion + process owner
S&OP Lead / IBP Director

Orchestrates the planning cycle. Their biggest pain: slow data, no single version of truth, mistrust across functions.

Opening Q: "How long is your S&OP cycle?"
Demand & Inventory Planner

Tactical. Lives in spreadsheets. Low forecast accuracy, poor external signal access. Wants clean, fast data.

Opening Q: "What's your forecast accuracy?"
Data & IT layer — internal Snowflake champion
CIO / Data & Analytics Lead

Platform consolidator. Drowning in point-to-point integrations. Wants to reduce data estate complexity and enable self-service. Often already a Snowflake customer — SC hasn't been connected yet. That's the fastest win.

Opening Q: "How many systems does your SC team pull data from to run S&OP?"

If Snowflake is already in the account, start with the Data Lead. SC team using it yet? That's your first meeting.

PERSONAS · WHAT TO SAY

Speak their language. Every persona has one thing they actually measure.

CSCO / VP Supply Chain
Exec sponsor
Metric they own: OTIF, cost-to-serve, inventory turns
Fear: Another disruption with no early warning
Your message: "Your planning tools are only as good as the data feeding them. We're the reason that data is clean, current, and connected."
S&OP Lead / IBP Director
Tech champion
Metric they own: Planning cycle time, forecast accuracy, plan adherence
Fear: Cross-functional mistrust; every team has different numbers
Your message: "What if Finance, Supply, and Demand all started from the same number? What would that do to your cycle time?"
CPO / Procurement Director
Exec buyer
Metric they own: Supplier risk, raw material cost variance, spend under management
Fear: Blind spots in the supplier network; another $0.08-chip moment
Your message: "How many tiers deep can you see into your supplier network today? We can make that 4–6 tiers, in near-real-time."
Demand & Inventory Planner
Process owner
Metric they own: Forecast MAPE, safety stock levels, SLOB inventory
Fear: Being blamed for stockouts or overstock with no data to defend themselves
Your message: "What external signals are you pulling into your forecast today? We let you blend POS, social, weather, and economic signals at scale."

Don't open with Snowflake. Open with their number. Then show how we move it.

Bluecrux
SC Design · S&OP · IBP Transformation

SC strategy and transformation consultancy. Strongest in S&OP / IBP design and SC network design. They work from process to technology — and they lead with the business problem, not the platform. Ideal for companies reinventing their planning model, not just upgrading their tools.

Bring in at: IBP transformation / SC redesign conversation
