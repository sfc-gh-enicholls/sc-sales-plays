Why now

Buys are committed six to nine months ahead, then the season is read in weeks. Planning, PLM, merchandising, ecommerce and store POS still sit apart, so the WSSI is rebuilt by hand and markdowns are decided late and deep. For EU-trading brands, ESPR adds Digital Product Passports and a ban on destroying unsold apparel.

What changes with Snowflake

  • Unify & govern. Planning, merchandising, PLM, ecommerce, POS and supplier data in one governed model. SAP without copying via SAP Business Data Cloud; Blue Yonder, Oracle Retail, Anaplan, PLM, ecommerce and POS through connectors, Openflow and Snowflake data sharing.
  • Reason & predict. Cortex AI and Snowflake ML on governed data for demand sensing, size curves, markdown and terminal-stock risk.
  • Decide & act. Cortex Analyst and Snowflake Intelligence answer trading questions in plain English; approved actions write back to planning and merchandising systems through integrations.
